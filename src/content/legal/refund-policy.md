# Cancellation and refund policy

**Last updated:** {{lastUpdated}}

This page explains how cancellations and refunds work for HireBuddha, a service of {{legalName}}. It repeats sections 8 and 9 of our [Terms of Service](/terms), which govern if the two ever differ.

## 1. How you pay

* HireBuddha charges for what your AI employees use (AI models, tools, phone minutes, messages and other services) at the prices shown in HireBuddha and on our [pricing page](/pricing), against your **Credits**.
* **Free daily credits**, when offered, expire at the end of the day they are given.
* **Credits you buy (top-ups)** are valid for 365 days from purchase.
* **Credits included with a subscription plan** follow the plan's terms.
* Credits have no cash value and cannot be transferred.
* Payments are made through Razorpay, in the currency shown at checkout. Prices exclude taxes; Indian GST is added where it applies.

## 2. Cancelling

* You can stop using HireBuddha at any time.
* **Subscriptions** renew automatically for the same period until cancelled. Cancel at any time in **Billing → Plan**; the cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period, and you keep access until then.
* To close your business account and delete its data, see [Data deletion](/data-deletion).

## 3. Refunds

Payments are **non-refundable**, except:

* a duplicate or mistaken charge;
* a charge for a service we failed to provide because of our fault;
* where the law requires a refund.

To ask for a refund, write to [{{contactEmail}}](mailto:{{contactEmail}}) **within 30 days of the charge**, with the e-mail address of your HireBuddha account and the payment's date and amount. We reply within 5 working days. Approved refunds are made to the original payment method, usually within 5–7 working days of approval, and the matching Credits are removed.

When an account is closed, unused Credits lapse and are not refunded, except where the law requires otherwise.

## 4. Third-party charges

Charges made by other providers, such as Google Ads spend or a model provider you pay directly with your own key, are between you and that provider, and are not refunded by us.
