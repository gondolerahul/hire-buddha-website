## The Problem

Executive visibility is broken. Your numbers live in six different tools. Your analyst spends two days pulling data, three hours formatting slides, and delivers a deck that's already stale by the time it reaches the boardroom. Strategic questions go unanswered because the data to answer them is scattered across CRM pipelines, accounting ledgers, support ticket queues, and HR dashboards.

The irony: the people who most need to see the whole picture — executives — are the ones with the least time to assemble it.

## The HireBuddha Solution

### Which Arc(s): Evolve (with data feeds from all other arcs)

### Agents Involved:
- **Chief Strategy Analyst** — Cross-process KPI harvesting and analysis
- **Executive Briefing Agent** — Deck and report generation
- **Insights Synthesizer** — Voice-of-customer and market signal analysis
- **Cashflow Forecaster** (Sustain) — Financial projections

### Step-by-Step Walkthrough:

**Sunday, 11:00 PM — Data Harvest:**
The Chief Strategy Analyst queries the execution database across all six arcs of Sheel:

- **Perceive:** 340 leads enriched this week. 12 competitive signals flagged. 2 regulatory changes detected.
- **Engage:** 1,200 outbound emails sent (28% open rate, up from 22% last week). 47 demos booked. 8 deals closed ($142K total). CSAT: 4.7/5.0. 3 churning accounts flagged.
- **Orchestrate:** 94% of dispatched tasks completed within SLA. 2 new AI Employees built by the Meta Agent.
- **Fulfill:** 312 orders fulfilled. 99.1% delivery accuracy. 1 integration self-healed (shipping carrier API change).
- **Sustain:** $847K received. $234K paid. Cash position: $1.2M. AR aging: 4 accounts 30+ days overdue. Month-end close: Day 1.
- **Evolve:** Instruction Optimizer tuned 3 agent charters this week. Outbound email reply rate improved 34% from Week 1.

**Monday, 3:00 AM — Analysis:**
The Chief Strategy Analyst runs cross-process correlation analysis:

- "Email open rate improvement correlates with the Instruction Optimizer's subject line tuning — confirming the Learning System is driving measurable sales improvement."
- "Churn risk in 3 accounts correlates with declining product usage, not support quality. Recommend proactive outreach from the Renewal Agent."
- "Cash position strong, but 2 large receivables aging. Recommend escalation to Collections Voice Agent if unpaid by Day 14."

**Monday, 5:00 AM — Briefing Generation:**
The Executive Briefing Agent compiles everything into a structured deck:

1. **Executive Summary** — 3-sentence overview of the week
2. **Revenue & Pipeline** — Deals closed, pipeline value, conversion rates, with week-over-week trends
3. **Customer Health** — CSAT trends, churn risks, expansion opportunities
4. **Operations** — Fulfillment accuracy, SLA compliance, integration health
5. **Finance** — Cash position, AR/AP status, 90-day runway projection
6. **AI Workforce Health** — Agent utilization, Learning System improvements, Meta Agent activity
7. **Strategic Recommendations** — Prioritized actions for the coming week

**Monday, 7:00 AM — Delivery:**
Pragya delivers the briefing to the CEO — via email, Slack, or a voice summary on their morning commute. The deck is also available in the dashboard.

**Total time invested by leadership:** 4 minutes to review. Zero time to compile.

### Expected Outcomes:
- **Briefing generation time:** Fully automatic (vs. 2–3 days analyst effort)
- **Data freshness:** Real-time (vs. 1–3 week lag in traditional reporting)
- **Cross-process visibility:** All six arcs in one view (vs. 6+ tools manually reconciled)
- **Cost:** ~$0.50–2.00 per briefing (vs. $1,000–2,500 analyst time per week)
- **Strategic quality:** Data-driven recommendations, not opinion-based slide decks
