## The Problem

Your support team is a bottleneck you can't scale. Every new customer adds tickets. Every ticket takes 5–15 minutes of human attention. Peak hours overflow. Off-hours go unanswered. Your best agents burn out. Your worst agents damage your brand. And every time someone leaves, their knowledge of your product, your customers, and your edge cases leaves with them.

The math doesn't work: you can't hire fast enough to keep up with growth, and you can't afford to hire enough to cover every channel, every timezone, and every language.

## The HireBuddha Solution

### Which Arc(s): Engage (Karuna)

### Agents Involved:
- **Omnichannel Care Orchestrator** — Primary support agent across all channels
- **Tier-2 Tech Resolver** — Handles technical issues requiring debugging or code-level investigation
- **Translation & Localization Agent** — Converses in the customer's language
- **Survey & NPS Agent** — Collects satisfaction scores after resolution
- **Reputation & Review Agent** — Solicits and responds to reviews

### Step-by-Step Walkthrough:

**Setup (10 minutes):**
Upload your knowledge base — product docs, FAQ, troubleshooting guides, policies. Connect your email inbox, WhatsApp Business, and phone line. Configure your Omnichannel Care Orchestrator's personality: empathetic, concise, solution-focused.

**Inbound Email, 2:13 AM:**
A customer emails about a billing discrepancy. The Care Orchestrator classifies the email (sentiment: frustrated, urgency: medium, topic: billing). It searches the knowledge base, finds the relevant billing policy, cross-references the customer's account history via Episodic Memory ("this customer had a similar issue in March"), and drafts a resolution — applying the credit automatically and sending a clear, empathetic explanation.

Resolution time: 47 seconds. Human involvement: zero.

**Inbound WhatsApp, 9:30 AM (in Spanish):**
A customer sends a WhatsApp message in Spanish asking about their order status. The Translation & Localization Agent detects the language. The Care Orchestrator queries the order management system, retrieves the tracking number, and responds in fluent Spanish with a delivery ETA and tracking link.

**Inbound Voice Call, 11:45 AM:**
A customer calls about a technical issue — their integration isn't syncing data. The Care Orchestrator takes the call (via OmniBuddha voice), asks diagnostic questions, and determines this requires code-level investigation. It transfers to the Tier-2 Tech Resolver, which accesses the customer's integration logs, identifies a configuration mismatch, walks the customer through the fix, and confirms the sync is working.

**Post-Resolution, 12:00 PM:**
The Survey & NPS Agent sends a quick CSAT survey via WhatsApp. Score: 5/5. The Reputation & Review Agent sends a gentle prompt: "Glad we could help! Would you mind sharing your experience on G2?"

### Expected Outcomes:
- **Resolution time:** <60 seconds for Tier-1 (vs. 5–15 minutes human)
- **Availability:** 24/7/365 — no off-hours, no holidays
- **Language support:** Any language the LLM supports — automatically detected
- **Cost per ticket:** ~$0.02–0.05 (vs. $8–15 human)
- **CSAT improvement:** Faster resolution + consistent empathy = higher satisfaction
- **Escalation rate:** <10% of tickets require human attention
