## The Problem

Your sales pipeline depends on people — SDRs to prospect, AEs to close, managers to forecast. Each hire costs $45K+ to recruit and onboard. Each one takes 90 days to become productive. Average tenure is 2.3 years. When they leave, their pipeline walks out with them — and CORTEX can't replace what was never written down.

Meanwhile, your competitors are already prospecting at machine speed. Every day you spend building a human sales team, they're widening the gap.

## The HireBuddha Solution

### Which Arc(s): Perceive → Engage → Orchestrate

### Agents Involved:
- **Market Intel Scout** (Perceive) — Enriches your ICP with firmographic, technographic, and intent data
- **Outbound Prospector** (Engage) — Multi-channel personalized outreach
- **Inbound Deal Closer** (Engage) — Voice-based qualification and closing
- **Proposal & Quote Agent** (Engage) — ROI proposals and contract routing
- **E-Signature Router** (Sustain) — Digital signature execution
- **Campaign Orchestrator** (Engage) — Multi-channel sequencing and optimization

### Step-by-Step Walkthrough:

**Day 1, 6:00 AM — Enrich**
Upload a CSV of 500 target companies. The Market Intel Scout enriches each record with firmographic data (revenue, employee count, tech stack), intent signals (hiring patterns, web activity), and contact information (decision-maker emails, LinkedIn profiles). 480 records are enriched. 43 score above your qualification threshold.

**Day 1, 7:00 AM — Outreach**
The Outbound Prospector drafts 43 personalized emails — each one referencing the prospect's company name, recent news (funding round, product launch, hiring surge), and a specific pain point mapped to their industry. Emails are sent from your domain, in your brand voice.

**Day 1, 2:00 PM — Multi-Channel**
LinkedIn connection requests are sent to the same 43 prospects. WhatsApp messages are queued for prospects with mobile numbers in regions where WhatsApp is the primary business channel.

**Day 3 — Follow-Up**
12 email replies. 8 positive. The Outbound Prospector sends personalized follow-ups to the remaining 35. 4 LinkedIn connections accepted — the Prospector sends direct messages.

**Day 5 — Qualification**
The Inbound Deal Closer calls the 8 warm prospects — by phone, in their language. Qualifies each against your ICP criteria: budget, authority, need, timeline. 5 are qualified. Demos are booked on your calendar.

**Day 7 — Proposal**
After 2 successful demos (conducted by you or by Pragya), the Proposal & Quote Agent generates two custom ROI proposals — with your branding, the prospect's data, calculated savings, and contract terms. PDFs are generated and routed for review.

**Day 10 — Close**
One proposal is approved. The E-Signature Router sends the contract via DocuSign. The prospect signs. The deal is closed. The new customer is handed to the Omnichannel Care Orchestrator for onboarding.

### Expected Outcomes:
- **Time to pipeline:** 1 day (vs. 90 days to hire and onboard an SDR team)
- **Cost per qualified lead:** ~$0.50–2.00 (vs. $50–200 for human SDR)
- **Pipeline generated:** 5 qualified opportunities from 500 targets in 10 days
- **Total cost:** ~$15–30 for the entire sequence (vs. $15,000+ in monthly SDR salary)
