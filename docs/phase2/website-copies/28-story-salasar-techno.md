## Company Profile

Salasar Techno Engineering Ltd. is a publicly listed company operating in the infrastructure sector. They specialize in steel fabrication, telecom tower manufacturing, and large-scale infrastructure projects across India. Their business development process is document-intensive: evaluating Request for Proposals (RFPs), developing technical and commercial proposals, negotiating terms, and processing tenders — all requiring deep domain expertise and significant analyst time.

**Size:** Large listed enterprise
**Market:** India — infrastructure, telecom, and engineering
**Challenge:** Accelerate the bid-to-win cycle across RFP evaluation, proposal generation, and tender processing without proportionally scaling the business development team.

---

## Before HireBuddha

Infrastructure bidding is a high-stakes, document-heavy process. Each RFP can be 50–200 pages of technical specifications, commercial terms, eligibility criteria, and compliance requirements. The traditional process:

- **RFP Evaluation (2–5 days):** Analysts read the full document, extract requirements, check eligibility criteria against company capabilities, and produce a bid/no-bid recommendation. During peak tender season, 10+ RFPs may arrive simultaneously.
- **Proposal Development (1–2 weeks):** Technical writers, engineers, and commercial analysts collaborate on a response — assembling technical narratives, project experience references, resource plans, pricing models, and compliance documentation.
- **Sales Negotiation (ongoing):** Client meetings, follow-up calls, term negotiations, and commercial adjustments require senior team involvement.
- **Tender Processing (3–5 days):** Compiling final tender documents with the correct format, signatures, earnest money deposits, and compliance certificates.

The bottleneck wasn't talent — Salasar had experienced engineers and analysts. The bottleneck was bandwidth: too many RFPs, too few analysts, and every day lost in the bidding cycle was a competitive disadvantage.

## The HireBuddha Deployment

### Arcs Activated: Perceive · Engage · Orchestrate · Fulfill · Sustain

### AI Employees Deployed:

**Document Processing Agent — "The RFP Analyzer"**
Ingests RFP documents (PDF, DOCX, scanned) using vision models and text extraction. Parses the full document into structured sections: technical requirements, commercial terms, eligibility criteria, timeline, evaluation methodology, and special conditions. Generates a structured analysis with a bid/no-bid recommendation based on configurable eligibility rules.

**Proposal & Quote Agent — "The Proposal Builder"**
Generates technical and commercial proposals from templates, pre-approved content blocks, and project-specific data. Assembles technical narratives, selects relevant project experience from the knowledge base, generates resource plans, and compiles pricing models. Outputs Word documents and PDFs in the required format.

**Outbound Prospector / Sales Agent — "The Negotiation Partner"**
Handles follow-up communications with procurement teams. Sends clarification requests. Discusses commercial terms via email and phone. Tracks negotiation status and escalates to human decision-makers for final pricing authority.

**Contract Review Specialist — "The Tender Auditor"**
Reviews final tender documents against a compliance checklist: are all required sections included? Are eligibility documents current? Are pricing formats correct? Are signatures in place? Flags gaps before submission.

**Market Intel Scout — "The Tender Tracker"**
Monitors government and private-sector tender portals for new opportunities matching Salasar's capabilities. Alerts the business development team to relevant RFPs as they're published.

## After HireBuddha

- **RFP evaluation in hours, not days.** The Document Processing Agent parses a 150-page RFP and generates a structured analysis with bid/no-bid recommendation in under 2 hours — versus 2–5 days of analyst time.
- **Proposal generation accelerated.** First drafts of technical and commercial proposals are generated automatically from templates and the knowledge base. Analysts review and refine rather than write from scratch.
- **Simultaneous bid processing.** Multiple RFPs can be processed in parallel — the AI workforce handles 10 simultaneous bids as easily as one.
- **Tender compliance confidence.** The pre-submission compliance audit catches missing documents, formatting errors, and eligibility gaps — reducing rejection risk.
- **Opportunity discovery automated.** The Market Intel Scout surfaces relevant tenders from multiple portals — opportunities the team might have missed during busy periods.
- **Senior team focuses on strategy.** Engineers and analysts spend their time on technical judgment and client relationships rather than document reading and formatting.
