## Company Profile

Eva World Tours is a travel agency offering curated domestic and international travel packages. They manage the complete travel experience — destination research, itinerary planning, booking, logistics coordination, customer support, and post-trip engagement. Their business depends on responsive customer communication across multiple channels and time zones.

**Size:** Small team
**Market:** India (domestic and outbound international travel)
**Challenge:** Manage growing customer volume across all business functions without scaling headcount proportionally.

---

## Before HireBuddha

Travel is a relationship-intensive, communication-heavy business. Every customer interaction is time-sensitive — a booking inquiry left unanswered for 4 hours means a customer booking with a competitor. Eva World Tours faced the pressure points every growing travel agency knows:

- **Customer inquiries flooded every channel.** WhatsApp messages, emails, phone calls, and social media DMs — all demanding fast, accurate responses about destinations, pricing, availability, and visa requirements.
- **Booking support was manual.** Itinerary changes, hotel substitutions, flight rebooking, and special requests all required human attention — and each one came with a time-sensitive deadline.
- **Financial operations were ad hoc.** Invoicing, payment tracking, vendor payouts, and commission reconciliation happened in spreadsheets.
- **Off-hours meant missed opportunities.** Travel inquiries don't follow business hours. A family planning their vacation researches at 10 PM. A business traveler needs an emergency rebooking at 5 AM. Without 24/7 coverage, these moments became missed revenue.
- **The team wore every hat.** The same people handling customer inquiries were also managing vendor relationships, processing payments, and planning marketing campaigns.

## The HireBuddha Deployment

### Arcs Activated: All Six — Complete Business Automation

**Perceive:**
- Market Intel Scout tracks travel industry trends, destination pricing, and seasonal patterns
- Social Listener monitors brand mentions and competitor promotions

**Engage (Karuna):**
- Omnichannel Care Orchestrator handles all customer inquiries — booking questions, itinerary changes, complaint resolution — across WhatsApp, email, and phone, 24/7
- Outbound Prospector reaches previous customers with seasonal offers and new destination packages
- Survey & NPS Agent collects post-trip feedback
- Reputation & Review Agent solicits and responds to Google and TripAdvisor reviews

**Orchestrate:**
- Service Dispatcher routes work between customer-facing agents and back-office operations
- Scheduling Agent coordinates travel dates, hotel check-ins, and activity bookings

**Fulfill:**
- Document Processing Agent handles visa applications, travel insurance forms, and booking confirmations
- QA & Audit Agent verifies itinerary accuracy before delivery to customers

**Sustain:**
- Treasury & Ledger Controller manages cash flow and vendor payments
- AR Agent handles customer invoicing and payment tracking
- AP Agent processes vendor bills (hotels, airlines, local operators)

**Evolve:**
- Executive Briefing Agent generates weekly business performance reports
- Insights Synthesizer analyzes customer feedback for service improvement opportunities

## After HireBuddha

- **24/7 customer responsiveness.** The Care Orchestrator handles inquiries at 2 AM and 2 PM with equal quality. No customer waits for business hours to get an answer.
- **Complete business automation.** Marketing, sales, customer support, finance, operations, and reporting all run on AI Employees. The human team focuses on high-touch customer relationships and curating exceptional travel experiences.
- **Proactive customer engagement.** Previous clients receive personalized outreach before peak travel seasons — "Goa monsoon retreats are available. Based on your last trip, you might love this itinerary."
- **Financial clarity.** Cash flow, vendor payments, and commission tracking happen continuously — not at month-end.
- **Reputation management.** The Review Agent actively solicits post-trip reviews and responds to both positive and negative feedback — maintaining a strong online presence.
