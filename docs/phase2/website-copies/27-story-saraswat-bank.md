## Before HireBuddha

Loan operations are the most operationally intensive function in banking. Two processes consume disproportionate resources:

**Loan Recovery:**
- Thousands of delinquent accounts require regular follow-up — calls, reminders, negotiation, and escalation.
- Human collection agents make 20–30 calls per day. To manage a portfolio of 5,000+ delinquent accounts requires a large team.
- Each call requires sensitivity — borrowers in financial distress need compassionate engagement, not aggressive tactics. Compliance requires specific language, disclosures, and documentation.
- Recovery rates are inconsistent — dependent on individual agent skill, persistence, and workload.

**Loan Origination:**
- New loan applications require document collection, verification, eligibility assessment, and follow-up.
- Applicants expect fast turnaround. Delays lose applicants to competing banks.
- Each application involves multiple touchpoints across multiple channels — phone, email, WhatsApp, branch visits.

Both processes are high-volume, compliance-sensitive, and heavily dependent on consistent human execution — exactly the profile where AI workforce automation creates the highest impact.

## The HireBuddha Deployment

### Arcs Activated: Engage · Sustain

### Loan Recovery:

**Collections Voice Agent — "The Compassionate Collector"**
Configured with Karuna's compassion as a core personality trait. The voice agent makes outbound calls to delinquent borrowers with a structured approach:

1. **Greeting and Identification** — Warm, professional introduction with required regulatory disclosures
2. **Account Status Review** — Clear, factual summary of the overdue amount, due date, and accrued interest
3. **Listening Phase** — The agent listens to the borrower's situation with empathy (VAD-powered pause detection)
4. **Negotiation** — Offers payment plan options within bank-configured parameters (installment amount, duration, interest waiver thresholds)
5. **Commitment Capture** — Records the borrower's commitment, sends confirmation via WhatsApp
6. **Follow-Up Scheduling** — Schedules the next check-in call if payment is promised

**HITL Governance:**
- All payment plans exceeding configured discount thresholds require human approval
- Any escalation to legal collection requires human authorization
- All calls are recorded and transcribed for compliance review
- Regulatory disclosures are embedded in the agent's charter — never omitted

**AR Agent — "The Reminder Engine"**
Sends multi-channel reminders before and after due dates — email, SMS, WhatsApp — with payment links. Escalates to the Collections Voice Agent when reminders are insufficient.

### Loan Origination:

**Outbound Prospector — "The Loan Advisor"**
Contacts pre-qualified leads (from the bank's database or partner channels) to discuss loan products. Explains eligibility criteria, interest rates, and documentation requirements. Schedules branch visits or initiates digital applications.

**Document Processing Agent — "The Application Processor"**
Ingests loan application documents (ID proof, income documents, property papers). Extracts structured data using vision models. Validates against eligibility criteria. Flags missing documents and sends reminders.

**KYB/AML Gateway Agent — "The Compliance Checker"**
Runs automated KYC/AML checks on applicants. Cross-references against regulatory databases. Generates compliance reports for the credit committee.

## After HireBuddha

- **Recovery contact rate multiplied.** AI voice agents contact hundreds of borrowers per day — versus 20–30 per human agent. Portfolio coverage went from partial to comprehensive.
- **Compassionate, compliant conversations.** Every call follows the same structured, compliance-approved script — with genuine empathy configured through the Personality Matrix. No aggressive tactics. No missed disclosures.
- **Multilingual coverage.** Borrowers are contacted in their preferred language — Hindi, English, Marathi, and regional languages — with culturally appropriate tone and pace.
- **Loan origination accelerated.** Pre-qualified leads are contacted within hours, not days. Document collection and verification happen in parallel. Time-to-decision reduced significantly.
- **Full audit trail.** Every call, every negotiation, every commitment, every payment plan — recorded, transcribed, and logged. Compliance review is effortless.
- **HITL governance maintained.** High-stakes decisions (payment plan exceptions, legal escalations) remain under human control. The AI handles volume; humans handle judgment.
