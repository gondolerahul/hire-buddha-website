## Company Profile

GoChillao.com is a B2B e-commerce platform specializing in corporate gifting. They connect businesses with curated gift suppliers, managing the full gifting lifecycle — from discovery and customization to ordering and delivery. Their clients include mid-size and enterprise companies looking for streamlined corporate gifting for employees, clients, and events.

**Size:** Small team
**Market:** India (multi-regional, multi-language)
**Challenge:** Scale sales and customer success without proportionally scaling headcount across multiple Indian languages.

---

## Before HireBuddha

GoChillao faced the classic growth bottleneck of an e-commerce marketplace:

- **Sales outreach was manual.** The founding team personally prospected, qualified leads, and pitched to corporate buyers — limiting pipeline to the hours they could personally invest.
- **Multilingual reach was impossible.** India's corporate market spans Hindi, English, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, and regional languages. Hiring sales reps for each language was cost-prohibitive.
- **Customer success was reactive.** Follow-ups with existing clients for repeat orders and seasonal campaigns happened only when the team remembered — or when clients reached out themselves.
- **Marketing lacked consistency.** Content creation, campaign execution, and lead nurturing happened in sporadic bursts between other priorities.

The team had the product-market fit. What they lacked was the operational bandwidth to exploit it.

---

## The HireBuddha Deployment

### Arcs Activated: Perceive → Engage

### AI Employees Deployed:

**Outbound Prospector — "The Sales Engine"**
Configured for multi-channel outreach: personalized email sequences, WhatsApp follow-ups, and LinkedIn engagement. Each touchpoint references the prospect's industry, team size, and gifting occasion (Diwali, employee appreciation, client onboarding).

**Inbound Deal Closer — "The Multilingual Closer"**
Voice agents powered by OmniBuddha, configured to handle inbound and outbound sales calls in Hindi, English, and Marathi. Qualifies prospects against GoChillao's ICP criteria, discusses pricing and customization options, and books demos.

**Campaign Orchestrator — "The Marketing Machine"**
Runs seasonal demand-gen campaigns aligned with India's corporate gifting calendar — Diwali, New Year, annual days, employee anniversaries. Segments audiences, sequences messages, and measures performance.

**Omnichannel Care Orchestrator — "The Client Partner"**
Handles post-sale customer success: order status inquiries, customization requests, delivery tracking, and proactive outreach for repeat orders.

### Timeline:
- **Week 1:** Knowledge base ingestion (product catalog, pricing tiers, gifting guides, company policies). Channel connections (email, WhatsApp Business, phone lines).
- **Week 2:** First outbound campaign launched. Voice agents live on inbound lines.
- **Week 4:** Learning System begins tuning outreach messaging based on response data.

---

## After HireBuddha

- **Sales pipeline expanded dramatically.** The Outbound Prospector reaches hundreds of prospects per week — in multiple languages — versus the dozens the team could manually contact.
- **Multilingual capability unlocked.** Voice agents converse fluently in Hindi, English, and Marathi. Regional language barriers that previously limited market reach no longer exist.
- **Customer success became proactive.** The Care Orchestrator now reaches out to existing clients before seasonal gifting windows — "Diwali is 6 weeks away. Shall I prepare your corporate gift catalog?" — driving repeat revenue.
- **Marketing runs continuously.** Campaign Orchestrator executes demand-gen campaigns on a consistent cadence — no more sporadic bursts.
- **The founding team focuses on strategy.** Instead of spending 60% of their time on sales calls and follow-ups, the founders now focus on supplier partnerships, product development, and strategic growth.
