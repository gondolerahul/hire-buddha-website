## Section 2 · THE FLOW

**Eyebrow:**
`FROM SIGNAL TO RELATIONSHIP`

**Section Headline:**
The complete customer lifecycle — without a single department.

**The Engage Flow:**

1. **Qualified Signal Arrives** — From the Perceive arc (enriched lead, churn risk alert, competitor opportunity) or from an inbound trigger (website form, phone call, email, WhatsApp message, social mention).

2. **Routing & Assignment** — The signal is classified by intent, urgency, and channel. The appropriate Engage agent picks it up — Outbound Prospector for cold outreach, Omnichannel Care Orchestrator for support, Inbound Deal Closer for sales calls.

3. **Engagement** — The agent engages the contact across channels — voice, email, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, chat — maintaining full conversational context through Episodic and CORTEX memory. Every interaction is informed by everything the platform knows about this contact.

4. **Outcome** — Engagement concludes in a business outcome: deal closed, ticket resolved, candidate screened, review solicited, campaign measured. Outcomes flow to the Orchestrate and Fulfill arcs for execution and to the Evolve arc for learning.

## Section 3 · MEET THE AGENTS

**Eyebrow:**
`YOUR WORLD-FACING TEAM`

### Outbound Prospector

**Role:** Multi-channel cold outreach — find, reach, and engage new prospects at scale.

**Personality:** Confident, warm, persistent but never pushy. Personalization-first.

**Channels:** Email · WhatsApp · LinkedIn · Voice (outbound calling)

**Tools:** Email Suite · WhatsApp API · LinkedIn Outreach · Calendar · Enrichment APIs

**What it does:** Receives qualified lead lists from the Perceive arc. Crafts personalized outreach messages referencing the prospect's company, role, recent news, and specific pain points. Sequences follow-ups across channels. Adjusts timing and messaging based on engagement signals. Remembers every interaction.

**Social Proof:** *GoChillao uses HireBuddha's Outbound Prospector to drive B2B corporate gifting sales — multilingual outreach across email and WhatsApp, personalized to each prospect's industry and needs.*

---

### Inbound Deal Closer (Voice)

**Role:** Qualify, quote, negotiate, and close deals by phone.

**Personality:** Professional, empathetic, solution-oriented. Configurable speaking rate, pitch, and warmth.

**Channels:** Voice (OmniBuddha / Gemini Live / ChatGPT Realtime)

**Tools:** Voice Engine · Sandbox Calculator · PDF Generator · E-Signature Handler · Calendar

**What it does:** Answers your business phone. Qualifies the caller against your ICP criteria. Discusses pricing, generates custom quotes in real-time, handles objections, and — with HITL approval where configured — closes the deal and routes the contract for e-signature. Full call recording, transcription, and sentiment analysis.

**Social Proof:** *Fortune Series — a real estate firm — uses AI voice agents for multilingual sales calling. Prospects are qualified, viewings are scheduled, and follow-ups are triggered — in Hindi, English, and Marathi — without a single human SDR.*

---

### Tech Pre-Sales / Sales Engineer

**Role:** Answer technical sales questions, run demos, and build prospect confidence.

**Personality:** Knowledgeable, patient, detail-oriented. Adapts depth to the audience.

**Channels:** Chat · Email · Voice

**Tools:** Knowledge Base Search · Sandbox Code Executor · Demo Provisioning

**What it does:** Handles technical objections during the sales process. Runs live product demonstrations. Generates custom technical assessments. Bridges the gap between what sales promises and what engineering delivers.

---

### Proposal & Quote Agent

**Role:** Generate ROI proposals and route contracts for e-signature.

**Personality:** Precise, professional, detail-oriented.

**Channels:** Internal (generates documents for human or agent delivery)

**Tools:** Calculator · PDF Generator · Word Generator · E-Signature Handler

**What it does:** Takes deal parameters, runs ROI calculations, generates branded proposal documents, and routes them for digital signature. Every proposal is consistent, compliant, and calculated — not hand-crafted in PowerPoint at midnight.

---

### Content Studio Agent

**Role:** Generate copy, images, and video for marketing campaigns.

**Personality:** Creative, brand-aware, prolific.

**Channels:** Internal (outputs to Campaign Orchestrator)

**Tools:** LLM (copy generation) · Image Gen (Nano Banana / ChatGPT Image / Kling) · Video Gen (Veo / SeeDance)

**What it does:** Produces campaign assets at scale — email copy, social posts, ad creatives, blog drafts, product descriptions, and video clips. All routed through the Intelligent Router for cost optimization. All consistent with your brand voice.

---

### Campaign Orchestrator

**Role:** Run multi-channel demand-gen campaigns end-to-end.

**Personality:** Strategic, data-driven, systematic.

**Channels:** Email · WhatsApp · Ads APIs · Social platforms

**Tools:** Email Suite · WhatsApp · Ads APIs · Scheduler · Analytics

**What it does:** Plans, executes, and measures multi-channel campaigns. Segments audiences. A/B tests messaging. Adjusts cadence based on engagement data. Reports campaign performance to the Evolve arc for cross-process optimization.

---

### Omnichannel Care Orchestrator

**Role:** Resolve customer support issues across every channel.

**Personality:** Empathetic, patient, solution-focused. Remembers every previous interaction.

**Channels:** Voice · Email · WhatsApp · Chat · Social

**Tools:** pgvector Knowledge Base · Email Classify · Voice Engine · Sandbox

**What it does:** Receives inbound support requests from any channel. Searches the knowledge base for answers. Resolves issues in seconds. Escalates only what it can't handle — with full context, so the escalation target never asks the customer to repeat themselves.

**Social Proof:** *Eva World Tours automated their entire customer support with HireBuddha — handling booking inquiries, itinerary changes, and complaint resolution across WhatsApp, email, and phone — in multiple languages, 24/7.*

---

### Candidate Voice Screener

**Role:** Conduct first-round phone interviews with structured scoring.

**Personality:** Professional, warm, fair. Follows a structured interview guide.

**Channels:** Voice (outbound calling to candidates)

**Tools:** Voice Engine · VAD · Scoring Framework · Calendar

**What it does:** Calls candidates, conducts structured interviews against your criteria, scores responses, ranks candidates, and shortlists the top performers — all with transcription and sentiment analysis. A human recruiter reviews only the shortlist.

## Section 4 · EXAMPLE SCENARIO

**Eyebrow:**
`IN ACTION`

**Section Headline:**
From cold lead to closed deal — without a single SDR.

**Day 1, 6:00 AM:**
The Perceive arc delivers 43 qualified leads to the Outbound Prospector. Each lead arrives with enriched firmographic data, intent signals, and a recommended outreach angle.

**Day 1, 7:00 AM:**
The Outbound Prospector sends 43 personalized emails — each referencing the prospect's company, recent news, and specific pain point. LinkedIn connection requests are queued for the afternoon.

**Day 3:**
12 replies. 8 positive. The Prospector books 5 demos on your calendar and routes 3 warm leads to the Inbound Deal Closer for phone follow-up.

**Day 5:**
The Inbound Deal Closer conducts 3 phone calls. Two prospects request proposals. The Proposal & Quote Agent generates two custom ROI documents with pricing, timelines, and projected savings — routed for review.

**Day 8:**
One proposal is signed via DocuSign. The deal is closed. The new customer is handed to the Omnichannel Care Orchestrator for onboarding — with full context from every touchpoint.

**Result:** Cold lead → closed deal in 8 days. Zero SDRs. Zero account executives. Zero spreadsheets. One loop.
