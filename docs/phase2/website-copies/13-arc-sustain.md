## Section 3 · MEET THE AGENTS

### Finance & Accounting

#### Treasury & Ledger Controller
**Role:** Reconcile transactions and close the books — daily, not monthly.
**Tools:** Bank Feed Synchronizer (Plaid/Yodlee) · SQL · Sandbox Code Executor
**What it does:** Syncs bank feeds. Matches transactions against the ledger. Identifies discrepancies. Generates reconciliation reports. Closes the books on a cadence you choose — daily, weekly, or monthly.

#### Bookkeeping & Reconciliation Agent
**Role:** Match transactions at the line-item level.
**Tools:** Plaid/Yodlee · pandas/openpyxl · SQL
**What it does:** Three-way matching of invoices, purchase orders, and receipts. Flags mismatches. Generates exception reports.

#### Accounts Receivable Agent
**Role:** Invoice, remind, and collect.
**Tools:** Email · WhatsApp · Voice · Payment Links · PDF Generator
**What it does:** Generates invoices. Sends payment reminders — escalating from email to WhatsApp to voice call. Includes payment links. Tracks aging. Reports overdue balances to Pragya.

#### Collections Voice Agent
**Role:** Negotiate payment plans for overdue accounts — by phone, with empathy.
**Tools:** OmniBuddha/Twilio Voice · Calculator · Payment Plans
**What it does:** Makes outbound calls to delinquent accounts. Negotiates payment plans within your configured parameters. Records agreements. Follows up. Handles the conversation with Karuna's compassion — firm but fair.

**Social Proof:** *Saraswat Bank deployed HireBuddha's Collections Voice Agent for loan recovery. AI-driven outbound calls negotiate payment plans across multiple languages — with full HITL governance on every commitment.*

#### Accounts Payable / Payout Agent
**Role:** Three-way match and pay vendors.
**Tools:** Stripe · Wise · PayPal · E-Signature · Invoice Matching
**What it does:** Matches vendor invoices against purchase orders and receipts. Queues approved payouts. Routes for HITL approval above configured thresholds. Processes payments via configured payout rails.

#### Payroll Run Agent
**Role:** Execute payroll cycles.
**Tools:** Gusto · Rippling · BambooHR
**What it does:** Pulls employee data from HRIS. Calculates gross/net pay, deductions, and taxes. Generates payslips. Routes for final approval. Triggers disbursement.

#### Cashflow Forecaster
**Role:** Project runway and cash position.
**Tools:** SQL · Sandbox Code Executor · Excel Tool
**What it does:** Analyzes receivables, payables, and revenue pipeline. Generates 30/60/90-day cash flow projections with scenario modeling. Alerts when runway drops below configured thresholds.

#### Tax-Matrix Agent
**Role:** Compute VAT, GST, sales tax, and withholding across jurisdictions.
**Tools:** Avalara · TaxJar · Calculator
**What it does:** Applies the correct tax treatment to every transaction based on jurisdiction, product type, and customer status. Generates tax-ready reports for filing.

### Legal & Compliance

#### Contract Review Specialist
**Role:** Audit contract risk against your legal playbook.
**Tools:** pgvector Legal Playbooks · python-docx · PDF Generator
**Reasoning:** Chain-of-Thought + **strict HITL** — every risk assessment requires human review.
**What it does:** Reads contracts clause by clause. Cross-references against your risk playbook. Flags high-liability terms. Generates redline recommendations. Routes findings for legal review.

**Social Proof:** *Durwankur AI Labs — India's first AI-native legal firm — uses HireBuddha agents for contract review as part of their SaaS product for lawyers. The same agents that power their business also power their clients' legal operations.*

#### Redline & Drafting Agent
**Role:** Draft and redline documents with tracked changes.
**Tools:** python-docx · PDF Generator
**What it does:** Generates contract drafts from templates. Applies redlines with margin annotations. Tracks versions. Routes for review and approval.

#### E-Signature Router
**Role:** Route documents for digital signature.
**Tools:** DocuSign · Dropbox Sign
**What it does:** Prepares documents for signature. Identifies signing parties. Routes for execution. Tracks status. Stores signed copies.

#### KYB/AML Gateway Agent
**Role:** Verify entities and individuals for compliance.
**Tools:** Middesk · Persona · Identity Verification APIs
**Reasoning:** ReAct + **HITL** — verification results are always reviewed by a human.
**What it does:** Runs KYB (Know Your Business) and AML (Anti-Money Laundering) checks on new entities, vendors, and counterparties. Generates compliance reports. Flags high-risk entities.

#### Regulatory Watchdog
**Role:** Continuous compliance monitoring.
**Tools:** Playwright · Alert System · Evidence Store (pgvector)
**What it does:** Monitors regulatory portals for changes. Cross-references new requirements against your compliance profile. Generates impact assessments. Alerts compliance officers. Maintains an evidence store for audit readiness.

#### Risk Register Agent
**Role:** Maintain and monitor the organization's risk register.
**Tools:** SQL · Sandbox · Dashboards
**What it does:** Tracks identified risks, mitigation actions, and residual risk scores. Updates the register based on new compliance findings, contract reviews, and operational incidents.

#### Fraud & AML Monitor
**Role:** Flag suspicious transactions and entities.
**Tools:** KYB/AML APIs · SQL · Sandbox
**Reasoning:** ReAct + **HITL**
**What it does:** Monitors transaction patterns for anomalies. Cross-references entities against watchlists. Generates suspicious activity reports. Routes all findings for human review.

### People, IT & Procurement

#### Talent Scout Coordinator
**Role:** Source and match candidates to open positions.
**Tools:** pgvector Resume Store · Email Ingest · Job Board APIs
**What it does:** Ingests résumés from email and job boards. Embeds them in vector space. Matches against job requirements by semantic similarity. Ranks and shortlists candidates for the Candidate Voice Screener in the Engage arc.

#### Onboarding Buddy
**Role:** Onboard new hires with guided workflows.
**Tools:** HRIS · PDF Generator · Slack
**What it does:** Generates onboarding checklists. Distributes welcome materials. Sets up accounts and access. Schedules orientation sessions. Checks in during the first 30 days.

#### HR / IT Helpdesk Agent
**Role:** Answer policy questions and handle access requests.
**Tools:** Policy Knowledge Base · Identity Provider · HRIS
**What it does:** Answers employee questions about policies, benefits, and procedures. Handles IT access requests — provisioning/deprovisioning accounts, resetting passwords. Routes complex requests to the appropriate specialist.

#### Procurement & Sourcing Agent
**Role:** Source suppliers, run RFQs, and raise purchase orders.
**Tools:** Web Search · Email · E-Signature
**What it does:** Identifies potential suppliers. Sends RFQ requests. Compares bids. Generates purchase orders. Routes for approval.

#### Vendor Onboarding Agent
**Role:** Onboard and qualify new vendors.
**Tools:** KYB/AML · E-Signature · Compliance Checks
**What it does:** Collects vendor documentation. Runs compliance checks. Generates vendor profiles. Routes for approval before the vendor is activated.

#### Access & Identity Agent
**Role:** Provision and deprovision system access.
**Tools:** Identity Provider · SSO · HRIS · Cloud APIs
**Reasoning:** ReAct + **HITL**
**What it does:** Manages user accounts across your systems. Provisions access for new hires. Deprovisions access for departures. Generates access audit reports.

#### Expense Management Agent
**Role:** Process expense claims and reimbursements.
**Tools:** OCR/Vision · Policy Knowledge Base · Payout Rails
**What it does:** Receives expense submissions. OCRs receipts. Validates against your expense policy. Flags violations. Routes approved expenses for reimbursement.

## Section 4 · EXAMPLE SCENARIO

**Section Headline:**
Month-end close in hours, not weeks.

**Friday, 6:00 AM:**
The Treasury & Ledger Controller syncs all bank feeds. 847 transactions matched. 12 require investigation.

**Friday, 6:30 AM:**
The Bookkeeping Agent runs three-way matching on all open invoices. 4 discrepancies flagged and queued for review.

**Friday, 7:00 AM:**
The AR Agent sends 23 overdue reminders — 15 by email, 8 by WhatsApp. 3 accounts flagged for the Collections Voice Agent.

**Friday, 8:00 AM:**
The Tax-Matrix Agent computes VAT/GST across all jurisdictions. Tax-ready reports are generated.

**Friday, 9:00 AM:**
The Cashflow Forecaster generates a 90-day projection. Runway is healthy. Two scenarios flagged for attention if a major receivable is delayed.

**Friday, 10:00 AM:**
Pragya surfaces a summary to the owner: *"Monthly close complete. 847 transactions reconciled. 4 discrepancies pending your review. 23 overdue reminders sent. Cash position: healthy. 90-day forecast attached."*

**Result:** Month-end close completed by 10 AM on the first day — not the last day — of the following month.
