## Company Profile

Durwankur AI Labs is a legal technology company that has done something no traditional law firm has: they've automated their entire business — marketing, sales, customer support, HR, finance, legal operations, and internal administration — using HireBuddha. But they didn't stop there. They built a SaaS product for other law firms, powered by the same HireBuddha AI Employees that run their own operations.

**Size:** Small, AI-native team
**Market:** India — legal services and legal technology
**Challenge:** Build an AI-native legal business from the ground up, and a product that lets other firms do the same.

---

## Before HireBuddha

Durwankur AI Labs was founded with a radical thesis: a law firm doesn't need to be built the traditional way. The founders believed that every function of a legal business — from client acquisition to contract review to compliance monitoring to financial management — could be run by AI agents, with human lawyers focusing exclusively on the judgment-intensive work that requires legal expertise.

The challenge wasn't convincing themselves — it was building it. Traditional legal automation tools handle documents or discovery. None of them automate the business of running a law firm. And certainly none of them could serve as the backend for a product that other firms would use.

## The HireBuddha Deployment

### Arcs Activated: All Six — Perceive · Engage · Orchestrate · Fulfill · Sustain · Evolve

This is the most comprehensive HireBuddha deployment to date. Durwankur AI Labs activated every arc of Sheel:

### Internal Operations (Running Their Own Firm):

**Perceive:**
- Market Intel Scout monitors legal industry developments, regulatory changes, and competitor activity
- Regulatory Watchdog tracks updates across legal jurisdictions relevant to their clients

**Engage (Karuna):**
- Outbound Prospector reaches law firms and corporate legal departments for SaaS sales
- Omnichannel Care Orchestrator handles client support across email and WhatsApp
- Content Studio Agent produces legal thought leadership and marketing content

**Orchestrate:**
- Service Dispatcher routes incoming work to the appropriate AI Employee or human lawyer
- Project Orchestrator tracks case timelines and deadlines

**Fulfill:**
- Document Processing Agent ingests and extracts data from legal documents
- QA & Audit Agent verifies deliverable quality before client delivery

**Sustain:**
- Treasury & Ledger Controller manages firm finances
- AR Agent handles client billing and collections
- Contract Review Specialist reviews vendor and partnership agreements
- HR/IT Helpdesk manages internal operations

**Evolve:**
- Executive Briefing Agent generates weekly performance reports
- Instruction Optimizer tunes agent behavior from case outcomes

### External Product (SaaS for Other Law Firms):

The Meta Agent built specialized AI Employees that power Durwankur's SaaS product:

- **Legal Contract Reviewer** — Clause-by-clause analysis against customizable legal playbooks
- **Compliance Monitor** — Continuous regulatory tracking for client-specific jurisdictions
- **Legal Drafting Agent** — Generates first drafts of standard legal documents
- **Case Timeline Manager** — Tracks deadlines, filings, and court dates

These agents run on HireBuddha's infrastructure. Durwankur's clients — other law firms — interact with them through the SaaS interface. The agents were built by the Meta Agent, not coded by software engineers.

## After HireBuddha

- **India's first AI-native legal firm.** Every business function runs on HireBuddha. Human lawyers focus exclusively on legal judgment, strategy, and client relationships.
