## What Pragya Does Inside the Product

On the website, Pragya is your guide. Inside the product, she's your operator.

Your AI workforce knows everything your company knows — from Day 1.

### Connecting Your Software
Pragya connects your existing systems: CRM, ERP, accounting software, HRMS, invoicing, telephony, email, WhatsApp, Slack, Teams. Each connection becomes a tool binding that any AI Employee can access. No API coding. No integration projects. Pragya handles the plumbing.

### Building Your Workforce (The Meta Agent)
This is where Pragya becomes something no human chief of staff can be. When you describe what you need — in plain language — Pragya commands the **Meta Agent** to build it.

The Meta Agent is the agent that builds agents. Here's the build loop:

1. **Goal Decomposition:** Pragya passes your description to the Meta Agent. It decomposes the goal into required capabilities, tools, data access, and governance settings.

2. **Tier Selection:** The Meta Agent determines the optimal tier: Action (single tool call), Skill (focused task), Agent (channel manager), Process (multi-agent workflow), or Loop (continuous function).

3. **Charter Writing:** The Meta Agent writes the system prompt — the charter that defines the agent's role, boundaries, personality, and behavior rules.

4. **Tool Binding:** Existing tools from the 20+ registry are bound. If a required capability doesn't exist, the Meta Agent generates it: production-grade Python code, sandboxed, tested, and registered.

5. **Simulation:** The assembled AI Employee is run against simulated inputs. The Meta Agent observes the simulation and checks for errors, edge cases, and governance gaps.

6. **Critique & Iterate:** If the simulation reveals issues, the Meta Agent critiques, adjusts, and re-simulates. The loop continues until acceptance criteria are met.

7. **Publish:** The finished AI Employee is published to your workspace — fully configured, versioned, documented, and editable in the No-Code Architect.

**Time elapsed:** 5–15 minutes, depending on complexity.

You described a goal. Pragya built an employee. That employee is now live, running 24/7, and learning from CORTEX.

### Managing Your Workforce
Once your AI Employees are deployed, Pragya manages them:

- **Monitoring:** She tracks every agent's performance — tasks completed, costs incurred, quality scores, HITL approval rates.
- **HITL Routing:** When an agent hits a checkpoint that requires human approval (a payout above threshold, a contract clause flagged as high-risk, self-evolving code ready for promotion), Pragya surfaces it to you — via the dashboard, Slack, Teams, WhatsApp, email, or phone.
- **Reporting:** She generates daily and weekly summaries — what happened, what's pending, what needs your attention.
- **Escalation:** If an agent encounters something it can't handle, Pragya escalates to you with full context — what the agent tried, why it failed, and recommended next steps.

### Learning and Evolving
Pragya is backed by CORTEX. Every conversation she has with you — every question, every instruction, every correction — is stored in persistent memory. She remembers your preferences, your business context, your decision patterns, and your communication style.

By Month 3, Pragya knows your business better than a new hire would after a year. By Month 6, she anticipates your questions before you ask them.
