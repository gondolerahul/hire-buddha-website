## The Problem

Screening is the bottleneck of hiring. For every role, 100+ applications arrive. Your recruiter spends 5–10 minutes per résumé, 20–30 minutes per phone screen. For 50 candidates, that's 25+ hours of manual screening — and 80% of those candidates won't make it past the first round.

The result: slow time-to-hire, recruiter burnout, inconsistent evaluation criteria, and qualified candidates who accept other offers while waiting for your callback.

---

## The HireBuddha Solution

### Which Arc(s): Engage (screening) + Sustain (talent sourcing, onboarding)

### Agents Involved:
- **Talent Scout Coordinator** (Sustain) — Sources and matches résumés
- **Candidate Voice Screener** (Engage) — Conducts structured phone interviews
- **Scheduling Agent** (Orchestrate) — Coordinates interview calendars
- **Onboarding Buddy** (Sustain) — Post-hire onboarding

### Step-by-Step Walkthrough:

**Day 1 — Résumé Intake:**
The Talent Scout Coordinator ingests 120 résumés from email submissions, job boards, and your ATS. Each résumé is embedded in vector space and semantically matched against the job requirements. Top 40 candidates are shortlisted for screening.

**Day 1–2 — Interview Scheduling:**
The Scheduling Agent sends calendar invites to the 40 shortlisted candidates — offering flexible time slots, including evenings and weekends. 35 candidates confirm.

**Day 2–4 — Voice Screening:**
The Candidate Voice Screener calls each confirmed candidate. The interview follows a structured guide:

1. **Introduction** — Warm greeting, role overview, interview format explanation
2. **Experience Questions** — Role-specific behavioral and technical questions
3. **Scenario Questions** — Situational judgment exercises relevant to the position
4. **Candidate Questions** — "What would you like to know about the role?"
5. **Close** — Timeline expectations, next steps

Each call is 15–20 minutes. The Screener:
- Speaks naturally with configurable tone, pace, and warmth
- Adapts follow-up questions based on candidate responses
- Detects pauses and thinking time via Voice Activity Detection (VAD)
- Records and transcribes the entire conversation
- Scores each candidate on predefined criteria (technical fit, communication, culture alignment)

**Day 4 — Shortlist Delivery:**
The 35 screened candidates are ranked by composite score. A shortlist report is generated:

| Candidate | Score | Strengths | Concerns | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Candidate A | 92/100 | Strong technical depth, clear communication | Salary expectation 15% above range | Advance to final round |
| Candidate B | 87/100 | Excellent culture fit, creative problem-solving | Limited experience with Python | Advance with technical assessment |
| ... | ... | ... | ... | ... |

Your recruiter reviews the shortlist (15 minutes), listens to select recordings, and advances 8 candidates to final rounds.

**Post-Hire — Onboarding:**
When a candidate is hired, the Onboarding Buddy takes over: generates the welcome package, distributes policy documents, provisions system access, schedules orientation sessions, and checks in during the first 30 days.

### Expected Outcomes:
- **Screening time per candidate:** 15–20 min AI (vs. 30+ min human)
- **Total recruiter time:** ~15 minutes reviewing shortlist (vs. 25+ hours manual screening)
- **Consistency:** Every candidate is evaluated against identical criteria — no interviewer bias, no fatigue effects
- **Speed:** 35 candidates screened in 48 hours (vs. 2–3 weeks manual)
- **Cost per screen:** ~$0.05–0.15 (vs. $30–50 recruiter time)
- **Candidate experience:** Flexible scheduling, professional interaction, fast feedback
