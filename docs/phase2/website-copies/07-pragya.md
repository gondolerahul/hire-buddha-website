## Section 4 · WHAT PRAGYA DOES INSIDE THE PRODUCT

**Eyebrow:**
`HER REAL JOB`

**Section Headline:**
This is what she does every day — for your company.

**Section Copy:**
When you move from this website to the product, Pragya doesn't change. She expands. The same intelligence, the same memory, the same Meta Agent — now applied to your actual business:

### Phase 0 — Meet
You talk to Pragya. You describe your business, your goals, your pain points. She listens. She asks clarifying questions. She builds a mental model of your organization.

### Phase 1 — Ingest
Pragya ingests your knowledge base. She connects to your files, SharePoint, Notion, Google Drive, and databases. She chunks, embeds, and indexes everything into semantic memory. Your institutional knowledge is now searchable by meaning.

### Phase 2 — Connect
Pragya connects your software. CRM, ERP, accounting, HRMS, invoicing, telephony — each integration activated and tested.

### Phase 3 — Model
The dynamic schema seeds entities from your knowledge base. Pragya maps your departments to the arcs of Sheel using the process blueprint.

### Phase 4 — Build & Assign
Pragya deploys agent templates from the library — or has the Meta Agent build custom AI Employees for needs that don't have a template. She assigns them to the appropriate arcs and processes.

### Phase 5 — Run & Report
Your AI workforce is live. Pragya monitors every process. She surfaces HITL approvals — via the dashboard, Slack, Teams, email, WhatsApp, or voice call. She generates status reports. She flags anomalies. She recommends optimizations.

She is your single point of contact. Not a dashboard you check. Not a Slack channel you monitor. A colleague you talk to.
