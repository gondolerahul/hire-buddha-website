## Company Profile

DnR Studios is a game development studio creating interactive entertainment. Their core competency — and their passion — is game design, development, art, and storytelling. Everything else — marketing, sales, customer support, HR, finance, legal, and operations — is overhead that exists to support the core creative work.

**Size:** Small creative team
**Market:** Global gaming market
**Challenge:** Run a full business operation around a creative studio without diverting creative talent to business functions.

---

## Before HireBuddha

Game studios have a unique operational tension: the work that generates revenue (game development) is creative, deep, and requires uninterrupted focus. The work that supports it (business operations) is constant, fragmented, and interrupts everything.

- **Community support ate into dev time.** Bug reports, feature requests, player questions, and community management demanded constant attention from people who should be building the game.
- **Marketing was an afterthought.** Social media, content creation, press outreach, and community building happened only when someone could spare the time — which was rarely.
- **Financial operations were a founder burden.** Invoicing, payroll, tax, and bookkeeping fell on the founders — stealing time from strategic and creative decisions.
- **HR and recruitment were slow.** Finding and onboarding new team members was a manual, time-consuming process that competed with development priorities.
- **Legal reviews bottlenecked partnerships.** Publisher agreements, licensing deals, and contractor agreements sat in review queues because no one had time to read them.

The studio needed a full business operations team. But hiring one would double their headcount and transform a creative studio into a corporate one.

## The HireBuddha Deployment

### Arcs Activated: Perceive · Engage · Orchestrate · Sustain · Evolve

*Note: The Fulfill arc is handled by the game development team themselves — actual game development is the one function that remains human.*

**Engage (Karuna):**
- Omnichannel Care Orchestrator handles player support — bug reports, feature requests, and community questions — across Discord, email, and social media
- Content Studio Agent generates social media posts, press releases, and community updates
- Campaign Orchestrator runs game launch campaigns and player engagement sequences
- Reputation & Review Agent monitors and responds to Steam, App Store, and gaming community reviews

**Sustain:**
- Treasury & Ledger Controller manages studio finances
- AR/AP Agents handle client invoicing and vendor payments
- Payroll Agent runs monthly payroll
- Contract Review Specialist reviews publisher and licensing agreements
- Talent Scout and Onboarding Buddy handle recruitment

**Orchestrate:**
- Service Dispatcher routes business tasks to the appropriate AI Employee
- Project Orchestrator tracks business milestones and deadlines (not game dev milestones — those stay with the human team)

**Perceive:**
- Market Intel Scout tracks gaming industry trends, competitor releases, and market opportunities

**Evolve:**
- Executive Briefing Agent generates weekly business health reports for the founders

---

## After HireBuddha

- **Creative focus restored.** The development team spends 100% of their time on game development. Zero business operations tasks leak into the creative pipeline.
- **Player support is 24/7.** The Care Orchestrator handles player inquiries around the clock — across Discord, email, and social media — with empathy and product knowledge that improves with every interaction.
- **Marketing runs itself.** Content creation, community engagement, and campaign execution happen continuously without pulling a single developer away from their work.
- **Business operations are invisible.** Finance, HR, legal, and administration run in the background. The founders review a weekly briefing and make strategic decisions — that's it.
- **The studio scales without corporate overhead.** DnR Studios operates with the business capabilities of a 30-person company while maintaining the creative agility of a small studio.
