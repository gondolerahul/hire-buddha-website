## Section 2 · HOW BILLING WORKS

**Eyebrow:**
`THE ECONOMICS`

**Section Headline:**
Transparent. Granular. Always in your control.

### The 3-Tier Wallet System

**1. Daily Credits — Free Every Day**
Every account receives $5.00 in free daily credits. They reset at midnight. They don't roll over. They let you experiment, explore, and run lightweight operations without spending a cent.

**2. PAYG Wallet — Pay As You Go**
Top up your wallet via Razorpay. Funds are valid for 365 days. Use them when daily credits run out, or for higher-volume operations. No subscription required. No commitment.

**3. Subscription Credits — Monthly Plans**
Choose a monthly plan for committed usage. Subscription credits include bonus allocations up to 40% — the more you commit, the more you get.

### The Billing Formula (simplified)

Every task has a base cost determined by the model, tokens, tools, and compute used. A small platform fee is added. You see the total cost in your execution ledger — per step, per run, per agent, per process.

**The router is the cost story:**
> Each step runs on the cheapest model that will succeed. Routine extraction goes to a low-cost model. Complex reasoning escalates to BabyBuddha or a frontier model. That's why HireBuddha undercuts a flat "always-premium-LLM" price while keeping premium quality where it counts. You pay frontier-model prices only on the 10–20% of steps that truly need them.

## Section 10 · FAQ

**Q: Is there really a free tier?**
A: Yes. Every account gets $5 in free daily credits — enough to run lightweight operations, explore the platform, and deploy your first AI Employees. No credit card required.

**Q: What happens when I run out of credits?**
A: Execution pauses. You're notified. No charges are ever incurred without sufficient wallet balance. Top up your PAYG wallet or upgrade to a subscription plan to resume.

**Q: Can I switch plans mid-month?**
A: Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.

**Q: Are there any hidden fees?**
A: None. Every cost is visible in your execution ledger — per step, per model, per tool. The billing formula is transparent: base cost + platform fee. No markup surprises.

**Q: What if I need more than Enterprise?**
A: Contact us for custom volume pricing, dedicated infrastructure, or white-label arrangements.

**Q: How does the Intelligent Router save me money?**
A: The router evaluates every reasoning step and sends it to the cheapest model that will succeed. Routine tasks go to low-cost models. Complex reasoning escalates to premium models. You pay for capability only when you need it — typically saving 60–80% vs. flat premium-model pricing.
