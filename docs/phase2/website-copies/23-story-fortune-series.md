## Company Profile

Fortune Series is a real estate firm operating across multiple Indian markets. They develop, market, and sell residential and commercial properties. Their sales pipeline depends heavily on phone-based prospect qualification, site visit scheduling, and relationship-driven follow-up — all requiring multilingual fluency across a geographically diverse customer base.

**Size:** Growing mid-size firm
**Market:** India (multi-state, multi-language)
**Challenge:** Scale outbound sales calling across languages without hiring a large call center team.

---

## Before HireBuddha

Real estate sales in India are phone-first. Prospects expect to be called. They expect to discuss pricing, floor plans, amenities, and financing options — in their language. Fortune Series faced the arithmetic problem every real estate firm knows:

- **Limited calling capacity.** A human sales rep makes 30–40 meaningful calls per day. To reach 500 prospects per week required a team of 12+ callers.
- **Language coverage gaps.** Hiring callers fluent in Hindi, English, and Marathi — each with real estate domain knowledge — was expensive and slow. Each language gap meant a market segment left uncontacted.
- **Inconsistent follow-up.** After initial contact, follow-up cadence depended on individual rep diligence. Warm leads went cold because the third follow-up never happened.
- **Data capture was ad hoc.** Call outcomes were logged in spreadsheets — when they were logged at all. Pipeline visibility was a weekly exercise in detective work.

---

## The HireBuddha Deployment

### Arcs Activated: Engage

### AI Employees Deployed:

**Inbound Deal Closer — Multilingual Voice Agent**
Configured with three language personas — Hindi, English, and Marathi — each with locally appropriate tone, pace, and cultural nuances. The agent handles inbound inquiries and makes outbound calls to prospect lists. It qualifies callers against Fortune Series' criteria (budget range, location preference, timeline), discusses available properties, answers pricing questions, and schedules site visits on the sales team's calendar.

**Outbound Prospector — Sequence & Follow-Up Engine**
Runs multi-channel outreach sequences: initial call → WhatsApp follow-up with property brochure → second call if no response → email with virtual tour link. Persistent but never aggressive — Karuna's compassion is configured in every touchpoint.

**Scheduling Agent — Site Visit Coordinator**
Coordinates site visit calendars between prospects and the on-ground sales team. Sends reminders via WhatsApp. Handles rescheduling.

### Timeline:
- **Week 1:** Knowledge base loaded with property catalogs, pricing sheets, floor plans, financing options, and FAQ responses. Voice personas tuned and tested.
- **Week 2:** First outbound calling campaign launched — 200 prospects across three languages.
- **Week 3:** Inbound lines activated. All incoming property inquiries routed to the AI voice agent.

## After HireBuddha

- **Calling capacity multiplied.** AI voice agents contact hundreds of prospects per day across three languages — simultaneously. The 12-rep call center bottleneck disappeared.
- **Every language is covered.** Hindi, English, and Marathi prospects all receive fluent, culturally appropriate conversations. Language is no longer a market constraint.
- **Follow-up is automatic.** Every prospect gets the full follow-up sequence — no lead falls through the cracks because a rep forgot or got busy.
- **Pipeline visibility is real-time.** Every call outcome is logged, scored, and visible in the dashboard. No more spreadsheet archaeology.
- **The human team focuses on closings.** Site visits, negotiations, and contract signing — the high-value work that benefits from human presence — is where the team spends their time.
