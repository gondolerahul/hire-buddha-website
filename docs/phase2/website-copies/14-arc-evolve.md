## Section 3 · MEET THE AGENTS

### Chief Strategy Analyst

**Role:** Harvest and analyze cross-process KPIs for strategic insight.

**Tools:** SQL (across all execution runs) · Sandbox Code Executor · pgvector

**What it does:** Queries the full execution database — every run, every outcome, every cost, every critic verdict — across the entire loop. Runs statistical analysis, cohort comparisons, and trend detection. Identifies the highest-leverage optimization opportunities.

**Key Output:** Strategic recommendations ranked by impact and effort.

---

### Executive Briefing Agent

**Role:** Write leadership briefings that drive decisions, not just report numbers.

**Tools:** PPTX Generator · PDF Generator · Excel Tool

**What it does:** Generates structured executive decks — not data dumps, but narrative briefings that answer: "What happened?" → "Why?" → "What should we do?" Includes visualizations, trend lines, and scenario comparisons. Delivered on cadence or on demand.

**Key Output:** Weekly leadership deck. Monthly strategic review. Quarterly business review.

---

### Insights Synthesizer

**Role:** Convert customer feedback, operational data, and market signals into strategic roadmap input.

**Tools:** pgvector (semantic search across all feedback) · Sandbox Code Executor

**What it does:** Aggregates voice-of-customer data from the Care Orchestrator, Survey Agent, and Review Agent. Cross-references with operational performance data. Identifies the gaps between what customers want and what the business delivers. Outputs prioritized product and process recommendations.

---

### Instruction Optimizer (Self-Optimizing Intelligence Engine)

**Role:** Automatically tune agent instructions from real-world performance data.

**Tools:** Learning System hooks · Agent Registry · CORTEX

**What it does:** This is the most advanced agent in the platform. It:

1. **Measures** — Compares each agent's outcomes against its KPI targets over time
2. **Hypothesizes** — Generates candidate instruction modifications that could improve performance
3. **Simulates** — Tests modified instructions against historical inputs in sandbox
4. **Validates** — Pre-Critic and Post-Critic audit the proposed changes
5. **Promotes** — If the change improves outcomes without degrading governance compliance, the updated instructions are promoted. The old instructions are versioned and rollback-safe.

**Key Message:**
> Your AI workforce doesn't just execute — it *edits its own playbook* based on what worked and what didn't. The intelligence is not static. It compounds.
