## Section 2: The 3-Loop Starter Pack

### The highest-impact loops for startups. Step by step.

---

### Loop 1: Growth & Customer Acquisition — 60 minutes

This is your revenue engine. In one hour, you'll go from zero to a fully operational outbound sales machine.

**Step 1: Design your Outbound Prospector (10 min)**
- Name your agent (or let Pragya suggest one)
- Set the personality: professional, friendly, or aggressive outbound?
- Tune the empathy and formality sliders
- Write a one-paragraph system prompt: who is this agent, what do they sell, and what's their style?

**Step 2: Upload your target list (5 min)**
- Upload a CSV of target companies (columns: company name, website, industry)
- Or enter 10 companies manually to start small
- The Market Intel Scout will enrich the rest (contact names, emails, phone numbers, firmographic data)

**Step 3: Connect your email (10 min)**
- Enter your SMTP and IMAP credentials
- Your AI Employee sends emails FROM your email address — it's you, amplified
- Test with a single email to yourself to verify delivery

**Step 4: Configure outreach templates (15 min)**
- Write 2-3 outreach templates (or let Pragya generate them)
- Each template has merge variables: {{company_name}}, {{prospect_name}}, {{pain_point}}, {{custom_hook}}
- Your AI Employee personalizes each email beyond the template — adding context from web research

**Step 5: Set governance (5 min)**
- HITL: approve the first 10 emails manually, then switch to auto-send
- Budget cap: set a daily maximum (recommended: $5-10 to start)
- Voice calls: enable or disable for first campaign

**Step 6: Launch (5 min)**
- Review your configuration
- Hit Deploy
- Watch the first emails queue and send
- Monitor opens and responses in real-time

**🎯 After 60 minutes:** Your Growth Loop is live. Emails going out. Leads being enriched. Replies routed to your Deal Closer.

---

### Loop 2: Customer Success & Support — 45 minutes

This is your support team. In 45 minutes, you'll have 24/7 customer support running across email and WhatsApp.

**Step 1: Design your Care Orchestrator (10 min)**
- Name your agent
- Set personality: empathetic, patient, solution-oriented
- Configure response tone: match your brand voice

**Step 2: Upload your knowledge base (15 min)**
- Upload product documentation, FAQs, troubleshooting guides
- Drag and drop PDFs, Word docs, or plain text files
- HireBuddha chunks, embeds, and indexes everything automatically
- The more you upload, the better your agent answers

**Step 3: Connect your support channel (10 min)**
- Connect your support email (IMAP/SMTP)
- Optional: Connect WhatsApp Business for instant messaging support
- Optional: Connect a Twilio number for voice support

**Step 4: Set governance (5 min)**
- HITL: approve ALL responses for the first 24 hours
- After review, switch to: auto-approve standard responses, HITL on refunds and escalations
- Auto-escalation: flag any ticket the agent can't resolve confidently

**Step 5: Launch (5 min)**
- Deploy
- Send a test ticket from your personal email
- Review the response
- Go live

**🎯 After 45 minutes:** Your first support ticket is resolved. Autonomously. In under 60 seconds.

---

### Loop 3: Intelligence Engine — 30 minutes

This is your brain. In 30 minutes, you'll have automated performance monitoring across both loops.

**Step 1: Connect to Loops 1 and 2 (5 min)**
- The Intelligence Engine automatically discovers active loops
- Select which loops to monitor (both, in this case)

**Step 2: Configure KPI harvesting (10 min)**
- Choose which metrics to track:
  - Growth: email open rate, response rate, qualified leads, cost per lead
  - Support: resolution time, CSAT, escalation rate, cost per ticket
- Set baseline values (or let the engine establish baselines from first week of data)

**Step 3: Set briefing schedule (5 min)**
- Daily digest (recommended for first month)
- Weekly summary with recommendations
- Delivery channel: email, Slack, or both

**Step 4: Launch (10 min)**
- Deploy
- The Intelligence Engine will deliver its first briefing tomorrow morning

**🎯 After 30 minutes:** Your Intelligence Engine is live. Tomorrow morning, you'll receive your first executive briefing.

## Section 3: What Happens After Launch

### Your first month. No hand-holding required.

**Week 1: Monitor and tune**
- Review HITL queue daily
- Approve or modify AI responses
- Tune personality settings based on customer feedback
- Upload additional knowledge base content as gaps are identified

**Week 2: Reduce oversight**
- Reduce HITL gates on high-confidence responses
- Growth Loop: switch to full auto-send if first 50 emails performed well
- Support Loop: auto-approve Tier 1 responses, HITL on Tier 2+

**Week 3: Add Fiscal Optimizer**
- Deploy the Treasury Controller for invoicing and reconciliation
- Connect bank feeds
- Start automated collection campaigns for overdue invoices

**Week 4: Full stack operational**
- Add Compliance Engine for contract review
- Intelligence Engine generating compound insights across 4+ loops
- Your AI workforce is measurably smarter than it was on Day 1
