### 🌐 Web Intelligence Suite

**Web Search**
Search the web using DuckDuckGo or Google Custom Search. Your AI Employees can research prospects, verify information, and discover competitive intelligence — autonomously.

**Web Scraper**

### 📧 Email Operations Suite

**Email Ingest**
Connect IMAP mailboxes. Your AI Employees monitor inboxes in real-time — reading, categorizing, and triaging incoming email automatically.
- **Used in:** Customer Success Loop, Growth Loop

**Email Classify**
Automatically classify incoming emails by sentiment (positive / negative / neutral), urgency (critical / standard / low), and intent (support request / sales inquiry / billing question).
- **Used in:** Customer Success Loop

**Email Draft**
Generate contextual email responses powered by your knowledge base. Brand-aligned tone. Customer-aware context via episodic memory. Drafted, not sent — unless your governance rules allow auto-send.
- **Used in:** Customer Success Loop, Growth Loop

**Email Send**
Send emails via SMTP. Full control over sender, subject, body, attachments. Tracks delivery. Integrated with HITL for approval workflows.
- **Used in:** All loops that generate outbound communication

## Section 3: Tool Detail — Featured Tools

**Psychological Principle:** *Concreteness Effect.* Abstract tool names mean nothing. Concrete examples — input, process, output — make capabilities real and believable.

### How tools work in practice.

#### Example 1: Web Scraper → Email Draft → PDF Generator

**Scenario:** GoChillao.com's Growth Loop researches a target corporate gifting buyer.

1. **Web Scraper** reads the prospect company's website, extracting their team size, industry, and recent events.
2. **Enrichment & Signal Harvester** (Apollo) pulls contact details for the procurement head.
3. **Email Draft** generates a personalized outreach email referencing the company's upcoming corporate retreat (found by Web Scraper).
4. **PDF Generator** creates a custom corporate gifting catalog tailored to their team size and budget.
5. **Email Send** delivers the email with the catalog attached.

Time elapsed: 90 seconds. Cost: ~$0.08.

---

#### Example 2: Voice (Gemini Live) → Email Draft → Calendar Orchestrator

**Scenario:** Fortune Series uses AI voice agents for real estate sales prospecting.

1. **Voice (Gemini Live)** calls a prospect who inquired about a residential project. Conversation runs for 8 minutes in Hindi — covering project details, pricing, and location benefits.
2. **Email Draft** generates a follow-up email summarizing the call and attaching a digital brochure.
3. **Calendar Matrix Orchestrator** checks the sales team's availability and schedules a site visit.
4. **Rich Communication Broker (Slack)** notifies the human sales rep: "Site visit booked for Priya Sharma, Thursday 3 PM. Call summary attached."

Time elapsed: 12 minutes. Cost: ~$0.25.
