## The Challenge

Your bookkeeper spends 20+ hours per month on manual reconciliation — matching bank transactions to invoices, categorizing expenses, and preparing P&L reports. Late invoices pile up because follow-ups are manual, inconsistent, and time-consuming. Cash flow projections are monthly at best, and often outdated before they're finished.

## The HireBuddha Solution

Deploy the **Continuous Fiscal & Asset Optimizer** to automate reconciliation, collection, and financial reporting.

**What runs autonomously:**
1. **Real-Time Transaction Sync:** Bank Feed Synchronizer (Plaid/Yodlee) pulls every transaction as it occurs
2. **Automated Reconciliation:** Matches transactions to invoices. Categorizes expenses by chart of accounts. Flags mismatches.
3. **Intelligent Collection:** Multi-channel payment reminders — email (Day 7), WhatsApp with payment link (Day 14), voice call (Day 21)
4. **Tax Compliance:** Calculates tax obligations via Avalara/TaxJar. Tracks filing deadlines.
5. **Cash Flow Projection:** Real-time 30/60/90-day projections updated automatically with every transaction
