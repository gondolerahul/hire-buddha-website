## The Company

**Durwankur AI Labs** is India's first AI-native legal firm — a company that exists at the intersection of law and artificial intelligence. They don't just use AI for efficiency. They were built from the ground up on the premise that AI can run an entire business.

Their dual mission:
1. **Legal services** — Client acquisition, case management, compliance, support, and operations — all automated
2. **Legal SaaS** — A product for lawyers that provides AI-powered contract review, legal research, and case management — powered by HireBuddha agents on the backend

---

## The Challenge

Building an AI-native legal firm means starting from zero — no established processes, no legacy team, no existing operational infrastructure. Most firms in this position would hire:
- Marketing & sales: 3–5 people
- Client support: 2–3 people
- Finance & accounting: 1–2 people
- HR & operations: 1–2 people
- Legal operations: 2–3 people

That's 10–15 people before a single case is handled. For a startup, that's ₹60–90 lakh in annual payroll before revenue.

Durwankur chose a different path: deploy, don't hire.

## The Deployment

**All 7 business loops. Running simultaneously.**

### Loop 1: Growth & Customer Acquisition
- Market Intel Scout researches target firms and legal professionals
- Outbound Prospector runs personalized outreach via email and voice
- Inbound Deal Closer qualifies inbound inquiries and books consultations
- Smart Proposal Skill generates custom engagement letters with scope, pricing, and terms

### Loop 2: Customer Success & Support
- Care Orchestrator handles all client communication
- Answers questions about case status, billing, document requests
- Resolves issues across email and WhatsApp — in under 60 seconds
- Episodic memory means the AI remembers every client interaction

### Loop 3: Operational Fulfillment Pipeline
- Tracks case milestones and deadlines
- Manages deliverable timelines (filings, document preparation, court dates)
- Ensures SLA compliance for client commitments

### Loop 4: Continuous Fiscal & Asset Optimizer
- Treasury Controller reconciles firm finances in real-time
- Collection Campaign Agent chases overdue client invoices
- Tax compliance automated via Avalara integration
- Cash flow projected weekly

### Loop 5: Regulatory & Compliance Engine
- Contract Sentinel reviews every vendor and partner agreement
- Compliance Monitor tracks regulatory filing deadlines, professional license renewals, and bar association requirements
- HITL on contracts with liability exposure exceeding ₹10 lakh

### Loop 6: Talent Vitality & Resource Alignment
- Manages recruitment for any human roles needed
- Voice Screener conducts preliminary screening interviews
- Onboarding workflow handles new team member setup

### Loop 7: Self-Optimizing Intelligence Engine
- Harvests KPIs from all 6 other loops
- Weekly briefing delivered to founders with cross-loop performance analysis
- Optimization recommendations: outreach timing, support tone adjustments, collection strategy
- Compound intelligence: by Week 12, the Intelligence Engine was identifying client behavior patterns that no human analyst would have caught

## The SaaS Product

**Beyond their own operations, Durwankur built their entire SaaS product on HireBuddha.**

Their legal SaaS product provides lawyers with:
- AI-powered contract review (Hindi and English)
- Legal research and case law analysis
- Document drafting and template generation
- Client communication management

Every feature is powered by HireBuddha agents running in the backend. The same Contract Sentinel that reviews Durwankur's internal contracts also reviews contracts for hundreds of lawyers using the SaaS product.

**This is the ultimate proof of concept:** a company that not only runs its own business on HireBuddha, but sells a product that runs on HireBuddha to other businesses.
