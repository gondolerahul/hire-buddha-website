## Section 2: The Flow

### From order to delivery. Nothing falls through the cracks.

**Step 1: Order Intake & Validation**
When an order is placed (via API, email, or manual entry), the Fulfillment Orchestrator validates the details — product availability, shipping destination, payment status — and creates a fulfillment timeline with SLA milestones.

**Step 2: Vendor Coordination**
For businesses that rely on suppliers, the Vendor & Quality Manager:
- Sends purchase orders to vendors via email
- Tracks delivery timelines
- Sends automated follow-ups if deadlines approach
- Escalates delays to humans via HITL

**Step 3: Quality Verification**
When deliverables arrive (physical or digital), the quality verification process runs:
- For physical goods: Inventory Oracle confirms receipt, logs condition, updates stock
- For digital deliverables: Automated review against specifications
- For services: SLA compliance check against agreed timelines and milestones

**Step 4: Delivery & Confirmation**
The Fulfillment Orchestrator:
- Triggers shipping via ShipStation/Shopify integration
- Sends tracking information to the customer
- Monitors delivery status
- Sends confirmation and satisfaction check upon delivery

**Step 5: Vendor Scorecard & Optimization**
The Intelligence Engine maintains vendor performance scorecards:
- On-time delivery rate
- Quality acceptance rate
- Response time to communications
- Recommendations for vendor rotation or renegotiation

## Section 4: Example Scenario — Eva World Tours

### Real story. Real operations.

Eva World Tours, a travel agency, deployed the Fulfillment Pipeline to manage their trip fulfillment process.

**Scenario:** A client books a 5-day Rajasthan heritage tour for a family of 4.

**Hour 1:** Fulfillment Orchestrator creates the trip plan:
- Hotel reservations confirmed via vendor email
- Car rental booked and confirmed
- Local guide assigned from partner database
- Welcome kit preparation triggered

**Day -2 (before trip):** Automated pre-trip package sent to the client:
- Detailed itinerary with maps and timings
- Emergency contact numbers
- Weather forecast and packing suggestions
- PDF travel guide generated via Document Factory

**During trip:** Daily check-ins via WhatsApp:
- "Good morning! Your guide Rajesh will meet you at the hotel lobby at 9 AM. Today's highlight: Amer Fort. Enjoy!"
- Real-time issue resolution if plans change (weather, closures)

**Post-trip:** Satisfaction survey sent. Feedback logged. Vendor performance updated (hotel 4.5/5, guide 5/5, car service 3.5/5 — flagged for review).

**The entire fulfillment process runs autonomously.** Human intervention only for exceptional issues.
