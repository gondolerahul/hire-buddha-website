## The Challenge

You receive 200+ applications per role. Your recruiter manually screens resumes, conducts 30-minute phone screens with the top 20, and produces a shortlist of 5. That's 10+ hours per role on screening alone — before the first real interview.

When you have 5 open roles, screening becomes a full-time job that prevents your recruiter from doing strategic work.

## The HireBuddha Solution

Deploy the **Talent Vitality & Resource Alignment Loop** to automate resume screening and voice interviews.

**What runs autonomously:**
1. **Semantic Resume Matching:** Resumes parsed and compared against job requirements using 768-dimension vector similarity. Not keyword matching — meaning matching.
2. **AI Voice Screening:** Top candidates receive automated screening calls via Gemini Live. Natural, conversational, multilingual. Role-specific questions.
3. **Structured Evaluation:** Each call produces a scored summary with strengths, concerns, and a hire recommendation.
4. **Shortlist Generation:** Top candidates flagged for human interview. Full call summaries attached.
5. **Interview Scheduling:** Calendar Matrix Orchestrator books human interviews automatically.

## Real Deployment

**DnR Studios** — Game development studio hiring Unity Developers, 3D Artists, and QA Testers. Received 200+ applications per role.

**How it worked:**
1. 200 resumes parsed and ranked by semantic similarity to role requirements
2. Top 15 candidates per role received screening calls from the Voice Screener
3. 18-minute average call duration — role-specific questions about technical experience, past projects, and availability
4. Each call produced a structured summary: score (1-10), strengths, concerns, recommendation
5. Top 5 per role flagged for CTO interview with full call summaries
6. 6 hires completed in 8 weeks — zero recruiter fees

**Results:**
- Time saved per role: ~10 hours of manual screening
- Recruiter fee savings: ₹3–5 lakh per hire (industry standard 15-20% of CTC)
- Candidate experience: professional, structured, consistent
- Time from application to shortlist: 72 hours (vs. 2-3 weeks manually)
