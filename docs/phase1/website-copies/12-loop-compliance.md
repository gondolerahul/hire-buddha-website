## Section 2: The Flow

### Compliance that never sleeps.

**Step 1: Contract Intake & Analysis**
The Contract Sentinel receives incoming contracts (via email or upload) and runs a comprehensive review:
- Scans for unfavorable terms, indemnification clauses, and liability exposure
- Identifies missing clauses (termination, IP rights, data protection)
- Compares terms against your approved playbook
- Generates a risk-scored summary with flagged items highlighted

**Step 2: Risk Scoring & Routing**
Every contract gets a risk score:
- **Low risk (0-3):** Auto-approved (if governance allows) or routed for quick sign-off
- **Medium risk (4-6):** Flagged items highlighted, sent to legal/management for review
- **High risk (7-10):** HITL triggered. Full analysis attached. Contract held until human approval.

**Step 3: Regulatory Monitoring**
The Compliance Monitor runs scheduled crawls:
- Business license renewal portals — flags upcoming deadlines
- Regulatory update feeds — identifies new requirements
- Tax compliance calendars — tracks filing deadlines
- Industry-specific regulations — monitors for changes that affect your operations

**Step 4: Proactive Compliance**
When a deadline or renewal is detected:
- Pre-fills applications and forms where possible
- Attaches required documents from your knowledge base
- Routes for e-signature via DocuSign if needed
- Sends HITL notification for final approval

**Step 5: Compliance Reporting**
Generates monthly compliance summaries:
- Contracts reviewed, approved, flagged
- Regulatory deadlines met, upcoming, overdue
- Risk exposure trends
- Recommendations for policy updates

## Section 3: Meet the Agents

**Contract Sentinel**
- **Role:** Reviews incoming contracts for risks, missing clauses, and unfavorable terms
- **Personality:** Meticulous, conservative, thorough. Errs on the side of caution.
- **Channels:** Background process (receives documents via email or upload)
- **Key Skills:** Clause Analysis, Risk Scoring, Redline Suggestions, Summary Generation
- **Tools:** PDF Generator, Knowledge Base (contract playbook), Chain-of-Thought reasoning

**Compliance Monitor**
- **Role:** Tracks regulatory deadlines, crawls licensing portals, flags renewals
- **Personality:** Vigilant, systematic, deadline-obsessed
- **Channels:** Email (alerts), Slack (notifications)
- **Key Skills:** Portal Crawling, Deadline Tracking, Application Pre-fill, Regulatory Digest
- **Tools:** Web Scraper, Headless Browser, Chronos Daemon (scheduled checks), E-Signature Handler
