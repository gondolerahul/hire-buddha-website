## Section 2: The 3-Step Quick Start

### Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

---

### Step 1: Meet Pragya — 2 minutes

Open the Pragya widget (bottom-right of every page, or click "Talk to Pragya").

Tell her: **"I'm a solopreneur. Help me set up my team."**

She'll ask 3 questions:
1. What do you sell?
2. Who do you sell to?
3. What's drowning you right now?

Based on your answers, she recommends which AI Employee to deploy first.

*Most solopreneurs start with Sarah (outbound sales) or Maya (customer support). Pragya will tell you which makes sense for you.*

---

### Step 2: Deploy Your First AI Employee — 8 minutes

Pragya walks you through setup in real-time:

1. **Name your AI Employee** — Sarah, Maya, Raj, or anything you want
2. **Set their personality** — use the sliders to match your brand's tone
3. **Upload 1-3 key documents** — your product overview, pricing page, or FAQ. Even one document is enough to start.
4. **Connect your email** — enter SMTP/IMAP credentials so your AI Employee can send and receive on your behalf
5. **Review a test message** — Pragya generates a sample outreach email or support response. Review it. Tweak if needed. Approve.

**Your first AI Employee is now live.**

---

### Step 3: Add the Rest Over Coffee — 5 minutes

With your first AI Employee running, deploy a second one:

- If you started with Sarah (sales): add Maya (support) or Raj (finance)
- If you started with Maya (support): add Sarah (sales) or Raj (finance)

Pragya handles the setup. You just approve.

**In 15 minutes, you have 2 AI Employees working for you. In the background. Right now.**
