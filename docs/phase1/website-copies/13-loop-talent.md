## Section 2: The Flow

### From job post to Day 1. Automated.

**Step 1: Sourcing & Screening**
The Talent Coordinator:
- Posts job descriptions across platforms
- Receives and parses applications
- Runs semantic similarity matching between resumes and your requirements (768-dim vector comparison)
- Ranks candidates by match score and generates a shortlist

**Step 2: AI Voice Screening**
The Voice Screener conducts preliminary screening interviews via phone:
- Calls candidates at scheduled times
- Asks role-specific screening questions
- Evaluates responses for clarity, relevance, and communication skills
- Generates an interview summary with scores and highlights
- Flags top candidates for human interview rounds

**Step 3: Interview Scheduling**
Calendar Matrix Orchestrator:
- Finds mutual availability between candidate and hiring manager
- Sends calendar invites with preparation materials
- Sends reminders 24 hours before

**Step 4: Offer & Onboarding**
The Onboarding Agent:
- Generates offer letters (PDF) from approved templates
- Routes for e-signature via DocuSign
- Once signed, triggers the onboarding workflow:
  - Welcome email sequence
  - Document collection checklist
  - System access provisioning
  - First-week schedule

## Section 4: Example Scenario — DnR Studios

### A game studio that automated everything except making games.

DnR Studios, a game development company, deployed the Talent Loop to handle all their recruiting — from developer sourcing to onboarding.

**The challenge:** Finding game developers, artists, and QA testers in a competitive talent market — without a dedicated HR team.

**The deployment:**
1. **Job descriptions** uploaded for Unity Developer, 3D Artist, and QA Tester roles
2. **Talent Coordinator** receives 200+ applications per role
3. **Semantic matching** ranks candidates against role requirements — technical skills, portfolio quality, relevant experience
4. **Voice Screener** calls the top 15 candidates per role:
   - Asks role-specific questions ("Describe your experience with Unity's ECS architecture")
   - Evaluates communication skills and cultural fit signals
   - Generates structured interview summaries with recommendation scores
5. **Top 5 per role** are flagged for human interview with the CTO
6. **Offer letters** generated and routed for e-signature
7. **Onboarding workflow** triggered — system access, Slack channels, first-week schedule

**Result:** DnR Studios hired 6 people in 8 weeks — with zero recruiter fees, zero scheduling overhead, and every candidate interaction documented.
