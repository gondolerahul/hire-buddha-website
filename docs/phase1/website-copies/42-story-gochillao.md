## The Company

**GoChillao.com** is a B2B e-commerce company specializing in corporate gifting. They help companies find the perfect gifts for employees, clients, and partners — from branded merchandise to premium hampers to customized gift experiences.

Their market is competitive, relationship-driven, and seasonal. Peak demand hits during Diwali, Christmas, and year-end celebrations. The companies that win are the ones that reach procurement heads and HR managers first — with the right offer, at the right time.

---

## The Challenge

GoChillao's growth was bottlenecked by sales bandwidth. Corporate gifting is a high-touch, relationship-driven sale:
- **Prospecting:** Identifying companies with upcoming events, celebrations, or employee milestones
- **Outreach:** Reaching procurement heads and admin managers — typically via phone
- **Qualification:** Understanding budget, quantity, customization needs, and timelines
- **Proposal:** Creating custom catalogs tailored to the buyer's team size, budget, and preferences
- **Follow-up:** Multi-touch nurturing across email, phone, and WhatsApp

Doing this manually meant GoChillao could reach maybe 50 new prospects per day. Their competitors — the ones with larger sales teams — were reaching 200+.

Hiring SDRs would cost ₹3–5 lakh per month per person. And onboarding them on the nuances of corporate gifting — catalog knowledge, pricing tiers, customization options — would take weeks.

## The Deployment

**Three loops deployed. Heavy investment in voice.**

### Loop 1: Growth & Customer Acquisition
**The revenue engine.**
- Market Intel Scout researches target companies — identifying HR heads and admin managers at mid-size and large companies
- Enrichment via Apollo pulls contact details, company size, industry, and recent activity
- Outbound Prospector sends personalized emails referencing each prospect's specific situation:
  - *"Hi Meera — I noticed your team at LogiCore just crossed 200 employees. With the festive season approaching, I wanted to share some ideas for employee appreciation gifts that teams your size love..."*
- **Multilingual voice agents (Gemini Live)** make outbound calls in Hindi and English — discussing product options, understanding requirements, and qualifying interest

### Loop 2: Customer Success & Support
**The relationship keeper.**
- Care Orchestrator handles all post-sale communication
- Order status inquiries answered instantly via WhatsApp
- Customization requests processed and routed
- Satisfaction follow-ups after delivery
- Proactive re-engagement for repeat clients before festive seasons

### Loop 3: Self-Optimizing Intelligence Engine
**The strategist.**
- Weekly briefings track outbound performance, conversion rates, and seasonal trends
- Recommendations for outreach timing (e.g., "Start Diwali campaign 8 weeks early — data shows early outreach converts 23% better")
- Email subject line optimization based on open rate data
- Channel effectiveness analysis (voice vs. email vs. WhatsApp)

## The Voice Advantage

**GoChillao invested heavily in multilingual voice agents.** In the corporate gifting market, phone calls convert 3× better than email. But phone calls are expensive — each SDR can make 50 calls per day.

HireBuddha's voice agents (Gemini Live) changed the economics:
- **5,000+ calls per day** instead of 50
- **Hindi and English** — switching mid-conversation based on the prospect's preference
- **Natural conversation** — not scripted IVR. Real discovery questions, product recommendations, and objection handling
- **Memory** — if a prospect says "call me back next month," the system schedules the callback and the agent remembers the entire previous conversation
- **Instant follow-up** — after every call, a personalized email with a custom catalog goes out automatically
