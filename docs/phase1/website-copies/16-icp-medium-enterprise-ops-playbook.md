## Section 2: The Recommended Deployment Sequence

### The order matters. Here's why.

Not all loops are equal in terms of risk, impact, and learning value. This sequence is designed to build confidence progressively — starting with the lowest-risk, highest-volume loop, and expanding as your team gains trust in the system.

---

### Phase 1: Quick Win — Week 1–2

**Deploy: Customer Success & Support Loop**

**Why first:**
- Highest volume of repetitive tasks — the impact is immediate and measurable
- Lowest risk — support responses, not financial transactions or legal contracts
- Fastest feedback loop — you'll see CSAT, resolution time, and escalation rate within days

**Governance setup:**
- Full HITL on all outbound communications (first 48 hours)
- Auto-approve Tier 1 responses after initial review period
- HITL on refund requests and credit adjustments
- Human escalation for any sentiment below 0.3

**What to upload:**
- Product documentation (PDF/DOCX)
- FAQ database
- Support playbooks and escalation procedures
- Brand voice guidelines

**Expected impact:**
- 60–80% of Tier 1 support tickets resolved autonomously
- Response time: < 60 seconds (vs. 4–8 hours previously)
- Cost per ticket: $0.04 (vs. $8–15 previously)

**Success metrics to track:**
- Resolution accuracy rate
- Average resolution time
- Customer satisfaction score
- Escalation rate
- Cost per ticket

---

### Phase 2: Revenue Engine — Week 3–4

**Deploy: Growth & Customer Acquisition Loop**

**Why second:**
- Directly impacts the revenue pipeline — high visibility to leadership
- Easy to A/B test: run AI outreach alongside your human sales team
- Generates hard revenue numbers that justify further deployment

**Governance setup:**
- HITL on proposals over a value threshold (you define)
- Auto-approve standard outreach emails after initial review
- HITL on all voice calls for first 48 hours
- Budget cap per campaign (daily and total)

**What to upload:**
- Target company lists (CSV)
- Product information and pitch decks
- Pricing documentation
- Case studies and testimonials

**Expected impact:**
- 3–5× increase in outbound contact volume
- 20–40% reduction in customer acquisition cost
- Lead enrichment in minutes (vs. hours of manual research)

**A/B test framework:**
Run the Growth Loop in parallel with your existing SDR team for 2 weeks. Compare:
- Contacts reached per day
- Response rate
- Qualified leads generated
- Cost per qualified lead
- Time to first meeting

---

### Phase 3: Financial Backbone — Week 5–6

**Deploy: Fiscal & Asset Optimizer**

**Why third:**
- Automates the most time-consuming back-office work
- Reduces AP/AR cycle time dramatically
- Frees finance team for strategic analysis instead of data entry

**Governance setup:**
- Full HITL on all payouts and disbursements
- Auto-approve reconciliation and categorization
- HITL on any expense categorization exceeding a threshold
- Daily reconciliation reports auto-generated

**Expected impact:**
- 90%+ reduction in manual reconciliation time
- Real-time cash flow visibility (vs. monthly reports)
- Overdue invoice collection accelerated by 40–60%

---

### Phase 4: Full Stack — Week 7–10

**Deploy remaining loops based on your company's priority:**

**Fulfillment Pipeline** — If you manage physical goods, SaaS delivery, or services
**Compliance Engine** — If you handle contracts, regulatory filings, or licenses
**Talent Loop** — If you're actively hiring or managing a growing team

Deploy in whichever order addresses your biggest current pain point.

---

### Phase 5: Intelligence Layer — Week 11+

**Deploy: Self-Optimizing Intelligence Engine**

Deploy this LAST. The Intelligence Engine needs data from other loops to analyze.

- Connect to all active loops
- Configure KPI harvesting
- Set briefing schedule (daily or weekly)
- First cross-process executive briefing generated within one week

**This is where compound intelligence begins.** The Intelligence Engine analyzes all loops, identifies cross-functional patterns, and starts tuning agent configurations automatically.

## Section 3: Governance Configuration Guide

### How to set governance for each phase.

| Phase | HITL Level | Budget Controls | When to Reduce |
|---|---|---|---|
| Phase 1 (Support) | All responses reviewed initially | Per-ticket cost cap | When accuracy exceeds 85% for 5 consecutive days |
| Phase 2 (Growth) | Proposals require approval | Per-campaign daily cap | When A/B test shows AI outperforms human SDR |
| Phase 3 (Finance) | All payouts require approval | Per-transaction cap | When reconciliation accuracy exceeds 95% for 2 weeks |
| Phase 4 (Full Stack) | Per-loop governance settings | Per-loop budget caps | Gradually, based on loop-specific performance |
| Phase 5 (Intelligence) | Auto-tuning requires approval initially | System-wide budget | When auto-tuning recommendations prove consistent |

**Golden rule:** Start with full HITL. Reduce gates only when the data justifies it. You're building trust, not rushing to autonomy.

## Section 4: Team Transition Guide

### How to run AI alongside your human team.

**Week 1–2: Shadow Mode**
AI handles tasks, but human team reviews every output. This builds understanding and identifies edge cases.

**Week 3–4: Parallel Mode**
AI and humans handle the same types of tasks independently. Metrics compared side by side.

**Week 5+: Supervision Mode**
AI handles the volume. Humans handle exceptions, strategy, and quality assurance. Roles shift from execution to oversight.

**Communication template for your team:**
> "We're deploying AI to handle the repetitive, high-volume operational tasks that prevent you from doing your best work. Your role isn't being replaced — it's evolving from execution to strategy, from firefighting to quality oversight. You'll have more time for the work that requires human judgment, creativity, and relationship-building."

**Role evolution examples:**
- Support agent → Quality auditor + escalation handler
- SDR → Sales strategist + relationship manager
- Bookkeeper → Financial analyst + controller
- HR coordinator → Culture & talent strategy
