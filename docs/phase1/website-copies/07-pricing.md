## Section 2: How Billing Works — The 3-Tier Wallet System

**Psychological Principle:** *Simplicity + Transparency.* The #1 pricing objection is "I don't understand how the costs work." A simple 3-tier system with a clear formula eliminates uncertainty.

### Simple economics. Total transparency.

**Tier 1: Daily Credits — Free**
Every account receives **$5.00 in free credits every day**. They reset at midnight — use them or lose them, they don't roll over. No credit card required. This is your permanent free tier.

**Tier 2: Pay-As-You-Go (PAYG)**
Need more than $5/day? Recharge your wallet anytime. Credits are valid for 365 days. Top up as much or as little as you need — $10, $100, $1,000 — it's your call.

**Tier 3: Subscription Plans**
For predictable, higher-volume usage, subscriptions give you bonus credits on top of your investment.

## Section 4: The Billing Formula

**Psychological Principle:** *Process Transparency.* Showing the formula builds trust. The visitor thinks: "They're not hiding anything."

### Every task. One formula.

```
Total Cost = Base Cost (LLM tokens + tool invocations) + Platform Fee (small margin)
```

- **Base cost:** The actual cost of running the LLM inference, calling external APIs, and processing voice minutes
- **Platform fee:** A small margin on top of base cost
- **You see the total before it runs.** No hidden fees. No post-hoc charges.
