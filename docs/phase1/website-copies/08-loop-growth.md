## Section 2: The Flow — How It Executes

**VISUAL DIRECTION:** A DAG flow diagram showing the end-to-end growth process from target list to signed contract.

### The complete growth lifecycle. One loop.

**Step 1: Market Intelligence**
The Market Intel Scout searches the web, scrapes company profiles, and enriches leads with firmographic data via Apollo or Clearbit. From a raw CSV of company names, it produces a fully enriched prospect list — industry, team size, key contacts, email, phone, LinkedIn, and technographic signals.

**Step 2: Outbound Prospecting**
The Outbound Prospector takes the enriched list and launches personalized multi-channel outreach:
- Emails crafted per prospect, referencing their specific business, recent news, or pain points
- Voice calls via Gemini Live — natural, multilingual, in your brand's tone
- LinkedIn connection requests with personalized messages
- WhatsApp outreach where appropriate

**Step 3: Inbound Qualification**
When prospects respond — by email, callback, or form submission — the Inbound Deal Closer qualifies them:
- Asks discovery questions
- Scores lead quality against your ICP criteria
- Routes hot leads to demo booking
- Nurtures warm leads with follow-up sequences

**Step 4: Deal Advancement**
For qualified opportunities, the Deal Closer:
- Books demos via Calendar Matrix Orchestrator
- Generates custom proposals via the Smart Proposal Skill (PDF)
- Sends proposals with ROI calculations tailored to the prospect's industry and team size
- Routes contracts for e-signature via DocuSign

**Step 5: Feedback & Optimization**
The Intelligence Engine tracks the entire funnel:
- Email open rates, response rates, and conversion rates
- Call duration, sentiment, and callback rates
- Cost per lead, cost per qualified lead, cost per deal
- Recommendations for subject line changes, call timing, and messaging adjustments

## Section 3: Meet the Agents

### Your growth team. Always on.

**Market Intel Scout**
- **Role:** Researches and enriches target companies and contacts
- **Personality:** Methodical, thorough, data-driven
- **Channels:** Background process (no customer-facing communication)
- **Key Skills:** Web Search, Web Scraper, Enrichment Harvester
- **What makes them special:** Doesn't just find names and emails — finds context. Recent funding, job posts, press mentions, conference attendance. The intelligence that makes outreach personal.

**Outbound Prospector**
- **Role:** Runs personalized outbound campaigns across email, voice, and messaging
- **Personality:** Warm, persistent, persuasive. Adapts tone per prospect.
- **Channels:** Email, Voice (Gemini Live), WhatsApp, LinkedIn
- **Key Skills:** Email Draft, Email Send, Voice Call, Follow-Up Sequencer
- **What makes them special:** Learns from every interaction. Week 4 emails have higher open rates than Week 1 because CORTEX has learned which approaches work for your specific market.

**Inbound Deal Closer**
- **Role:** Qualifies inbound leads, handles callbacks, books demos, drafts proposals
- **Personality:** Confident, empathetic, solution-oriented
- **Channels:** Voice, Email, Calendar
- **Key Skills:** Lead Qualification, Smart Proposal, Calendar Scheduling
- **What makes them special:** Remembers every prospect. If someone who called 3 weeks ago calls back, the Deal Closer references the previous conversation, the proposal sent, and any concerns raised.

## Section 4: Example Scenario — GoChillao.com

### Real story. Real results.

GoChillao.com is a B2B e-commerce company specializing in corporate gifting. They deployed the Growth Loop to automate their entire outbound sales pipeline.

**Day 1:** A CSV of 500 target companies is uploaded — HR heads and admin managers at mid-size companies.

**Hour 2:** The Market Intel Scout has enriched 480 records. Company websites scraped. LinkedIn profiles matched. Contact details verified via Apollo. 20 records flagged as unverifiable and quarantined.

**Day 2:** The Outbound Prospector launches the first campaign:
- 200 personalized emails referencing each company's size, industry, and upcoming festive season
- 50 voice calls in Hindi and English — each call adapted to the prospect's role and company profile
- WhatsApp messages to contacts where mobile numbers are available

**Day 5:** 42 email replies received. 18 callbacks from voice contacts. The Inbound Deal Closer qualifies each:
- 28 marked as "interested — send catalog"
- 12 marked as "warm — schedule call"
- 4 booked demos for the following week

**Week 2:** Custom corporate gifting catalogs (PDF) generated for 28 interested prospects. 12 follow-up calls completed. 3 proposals drafted with volume pricing. 1 contract sent for e-signature.

**Cost for the entire campaign:** Less than the cost of one SDR's weekly salary.

## Section 5: Configuration Options

### Your loop. Your rules.

**Governance Settings:**
- Max daily outbound volume (emails, calls, messages)
- HITL on prospects above a value threshold
- HITL on proposals before sending
- Auto-approve standard outreach; require approval for custom proposals

**Personality Tuning:**
- Outbound Prospector tone: Professional ↔ Friendly ↔ Aggressive
- Deal Closer empathy level: 0.0 – 1.0
- Follow-up persistence: Conservative ↔ Moderate ↔ Relentless

**Tool Bindings:**
- Enable/disable voice calls per campaign
- Choose enrichment providers (Apollo, Clearbit, ZoomInfo)
- Select CRM integration for lead syncing
