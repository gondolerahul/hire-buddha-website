## Section 2: The Flow

### Your financial backbone. Always current.

**Step 1: Transaction Sync**
Bank Feed Synchronizer (Plaid/Yodlee) pulls transactions in real-time. Every deposit, withdrawal, and transfer is captured and categorized automatically.

**Step 2: Reconciliation**
The Treasury & Ledger Controller matches bank transactions against your ledger:
- Identifies matched pairs (invoice → payment received)
- Flags unmatched transactions for review
- Categorizes expenses by chart of accounts
- Generates reconciliation reports (Excel)

**Step 3: Accounts Receivable**
The Collection Campaign Agent handles overdue invoices:
- Sends polite payment reminders via email on Day 7
- Follows up via WhatsApp on Day 14 with a payment link
- Escalates to a phone call on Day 21
- Generates aging reports for management review

**Step 4: Accounts Payable**
The Tax & Disbursement Clerk:
- Processes approved payables via Stripe, Wise, or PayPal
- Calculates tax withholdings via Avalara/TaxJar
- Routes high-value payments through HITL for approval
- Maintains a complete payment audit trail

**Step 5: Cash Flow Projection**
Weekly projections generated automatically:
- Upcoming receivables (what's due to you)
- Upcoming payables (what you owe)
- Net cash position over 30/60/90 days
- Anomaly alerts (unusual spending, declining receipts)

## Section 4: Example Scenario — Saraswat Bank

### Real story. Real recovery.

Saraswat Bank, one of India's leading multistate cooperative banks, deployed the Fiscal Optimizer for their loan recovery operations.

**The challenge:** Thousands of overdue loan accounts across multiple branches, requiring persistent follow-up in multiple languages (Hindi, Marathi, English).

**The deployment:**
- Collection Campaign Agent configured with bank's compliance-approved scripts
- Voice agents (Gemini Live) deployed for multilingual outbound calls
- HITL configured for any payment arrangement exceeding ₹1 lakh
- WhatsApp integration for sending payment links and receipt confirmations

**The process:**
1. Daily batch of overdue accounts loaded from the bank's core system
2. AI voice agents call each borrower — in their preferred language
3. Agents explain the outstanding amount, due date, and payment options
4. For borrowers willing to pay: payment link sent via WhatsApp, confirmation logged
5. For borrowers requesting arrangements: agent negotiates within pre-approved parameters
6. Arrangements exceeding ₹1 lakh → HITL notification to branch manager for approval
7. All interactions logged with full transcripts for regulatory compliance

**Result:** Thousands of recovery calls processed with complete audit trails, multilingual support, and consistent compliance — at a fraction of the cost of a human call center.
