## Section 2: The Flow

### The loop that optimizes every other loop.

**Step 1: KPI Harvesting**
The KPI Harvester collects performance data from all active loops:
- **Growth:** Email open rates, response rates, call conversion, cost per lead, cost per acquisition
- **Customer Success:** Resolution time, CSAT, first-contact resolution, escalation rate
- **Fulfillment:** SLA compliance, delivery time, vendor scores
- **Finance:** Collection rate, days sales outstanding, cash flow accuracy
- **Compliance:** Contracts reviewed, risks flagged, deadlines met
- **Talent:** Application volume, screening conversion, time-to-hire
- All metrics timestamped and trended over time

**Step 2: Trend Analysis**
The Chief Strategy Analyst processes the data:
- Identifies trends (improving, declining, stable)
- Detects correlations across loops ("When email open rates drop, callback conversion rises — shift more budget to voice")
- Calculates period-over-period changes
- Benchmarks against baselines

**Step 3: Optimization Recommendations**
Based on trend analysis, the Intelligence Engine generates actionable recommendations:
- "Email open rates dropped 12% this week. Recommendation: Switch from question-based to benefit-based subject lines for the next outreach batch."
- "Support resolution time improved 23% after knowledge base update on product X. Consider similar updates for products Y and Z."
- "Vendor A's delivery reliability dropped below 80%. Recommendation: Shift 30% of orders to Vendor B."

**Step 4: Auto-Tuning**
With permission (configurable), the Intelligence Engine can automatically apply recommendations:
- Update the Outbound Prospector's subject line strategy
- Adjust the Care Orchestrator's empathy settings based on CSAT data
- Modify the Collection Agent's follow-up timing based on payment response patterns

**Step 5: Executive Briefing**
The Executive Summary Agent generates a structured briefing:
- **What happened this period:** Key metrics across all loops
- **What's working:** Improvements with contributing factors
- **What needs attention:** Declining metrics with root cause analysis
- **Recommendations:** Specific, actionable next steps
- **Cost analysis:** Total AI operations cost vs. estimated human equivalent cost

Delivered as PDF, email summary, or Slack message — on whatever schedule you set.
