## Sarah — Your Outbound Prospector

### "She finds your customers. You build your product."

**Who she is:**
Sarah is your AI sales rep. She researches leads, writes personalized outreach, follows up with prospects, makes voice calls, books demos, and never takes a day off. She operates across email, voice, WhatsApp, and LinkedIn — all in your voice, representing your brand.

**Her personality:**
Warm but persistent. Professional without being stiff. She adapts her tone to match each prospect — more formal for enterprise contacts, more casual for startup founders. She's patient with cold leads and enthusiastic with warm ones.

**What she does every day:**
- Enriches 50–200 new leads (company research, contact details, firmographic data)
- Sends personalized outreach emails — not templates, but unique emails that reference the prospect's specific situation
- Makes 20–50 voice calls via Gemini Live — natural, multilingual, in your brand's tone
- Follows up with non-responders at optimal intervals
- Qualifies callbacks and books demos

**Day 1 vs. Day 30:**

*Day 1:* Sarah follows your outreach templates precisely. She sends 50 emails and makes 20 calls. Professional, accurate, but generic.

*Day 30:* Sarah has learned which subject lines get opened (benefit-based outperform question-based by 18% for your market). She knows which prospects respond to empathy vs. data. She's learned that Tuesday mornings get the best callback rate. Her emails are better than yours now — because CORTEX has accumulated 30 days of pattern data that no human could consciously track.

**You control:**
- Tone slider (Professional ↔ Friendly ↔ Bold)
- Maximum daily outreach volume
- HITL on high-value prospects
- Daily budget cap
- Voice calls enabled/disabled

**Powered by:** Growth & Customer Acquisition Loop → Outbound Prospector Agent
**Her tools:** Web Search, Web Scraper, Enrichment Harvester (Apollo), Email Suite, Voice (Gemini Live)

## The Strategy Analyst — Your Monday Morning Briefing

### "No name. No personality. Just clarity."

**Who it is:**
The Strategy Analyst doesn't have a name or a face. It has a job: deliver a weekly briefing every Monday at 7 AM that tells you exactly what happened, what's working, what's not, and what to do next.

**What you receive:**
- **This week's KPIs:** Leads generated, tickets resolved, revenue collected, cash flow position
- **Trend analysis:** What improved (email open rates +12%), what declined (WhatsApp response rate -8%)
- **Specific recommendations:** "Switch outreach subject lines from question-based to benefit-based — data shows 18% higher open rate"
- **Cost breakdown:** What you spent on AI operations vs. what equivalent human effort would have cost
- **One action item:** The single most impactful thing you should do this week

**You control:**
- Briefing frequency (daily or weekly)
- KPI selection (which metrics matter to you)
- Delivery channel (email, Slack, or both)

**Powered by:** Self-Optimizing Intelligence Engine → KPI Harvester + Chief Strategy Analyst + Executive Summary Agent
