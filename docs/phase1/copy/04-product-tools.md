## SECTION 3 — Tool Detail Pattern (top tools)

**Principle:** *Demonstrated specificity.* For a handful of flagship tools, showing the input → output makes the capability undeniable and concrete.

**Realtime Voice (OmniBuddha)**
- *What it does:* Holds a natural phone conversation, speech-to-speech, with barge-in and sub-second latency.
- *How it works:* Bidirectional PCM streaming over secure WebSockets in 20ms chunks; voice activity detection tuned so it never talks over the customer.
- *In context:* An Engage-arc SDR dials 200 leads, qualifies them live, and books demos – in your brand's tone.

**Global Payout Rails**
- *What it does:* Executes vendor payouts and multi-currency contractor clearings.
- *How it works:* Stripe / Wise / PayPal APIs, gated behind a HITL checkpoint you control.
- *In context:* The Sustain arc validates a supplier invoice, then disburses payment – pausing for your approval over $X.

**PDF Generator + Automated Tax Matrix**
- *What it does:* Produces a compliant, branded invoice with correct localized tax.
- *How it works:* Avalara/TaxJar computes the jurisdiction's tax; WeasyPrint renders it to PDF.
- *In context:* Close a contract and the invoice is generated, taxed, and sent before the human would have opened the template.
