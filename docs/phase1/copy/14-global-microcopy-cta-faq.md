## 7. FAQ (objection-killers — place on Homepage, Pricing, and Enterprise)

**Is this just a chatbot with a wrapper?**
No. Each AI Employee is a 5-tier cognitive worker (Action → Skill → Agent → Process → Loop) running an 8-stage loop that audits its own decisions before and after every action, backed by a 4-tier memory system including CORTEX. It reasons, calls tools, remembers, and improves. A chatbot does none of that.

**Will it hallucinate or go off the rails?**
A Pre-Critic audits every action before it runs; a Post-Critic checks for drift after. You define HITL checkpoints where it must stop and ask your permission, and credit circuit breakers cap spend automatically. Autonomy is something you grant, checkpoint by checkpoint.

**Isn't AI expensive at scale?**
The opposite, here. An Intelligent Router sends each step to the cheapest model that will succeed. Since 80–90% of steps don't need a frontier model, your real bill lands far below a flat "always-premium-LLM" price, with premium quality where it counts. You see the cost of every task before it runs.

**How long does setup take?**
Around 10 minutes for your first AI Employee, often less. Talk to Pragya, describe the outcome, and the Meta Agent builds it for you. No code. No services engagement.

**Is my data secure?**
AES-256-GCM encryption, master key stored outside the database, stateless JWT auth, and strict multi-tenant isolation. You can restrict which models touch your data, set residency controls, and audit every model call. *(See [Security & Governance](05-product-security.md).)*

**What if it doesn't work for us?**
Start with one Sheel arc, prove ROI in weeks, and expand only on evidence. Everything is versioned and reversible. There's no rip-and-replace and nothing to unwind if you stop.

**My business is unusual. Will generic AI even fit?**
It isn't generic. A dynamic per-tenant schema and CORTEX memory learn *your* business; self-evolving code builds the tools you specifically need. The product adapts to you, not the reverse.

**Do I have to learn a complicated platform?**
No. You talk to Pragya, your single point of contact. She runs the platform on your behalf and surfaces only the controls you need, when you need them.
