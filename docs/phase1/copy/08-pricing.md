## SECTION 2 — How Billing Works

**Principle:** *Transparency as trust.* Buyers brace for hidden fees. Showing the actual formula and the wallet system pre-empts suspicion – and the router story turns "AI is expensive" into "AI is cheaper here."

## Three ways to fund your workforce.

1. **Daily Credits** – every account gets $5.00 of free credits each day. They reset at midnight and don't roll over. Enough to run real work, free, forever.
2. **PAYG Wallet** – top up as you go. Funds are valid for 365 days. Pay only for what you use.
3. **Subscription Credits** – monthly plans (Starter, Growth, Enterprise) with up to 40% bonus credits.

**The billing formula, in plain English:**
Every task has a base cost. We add a small, visible platform fee. You see the total *before* it runs. Voice is priced per minute, email per send, documents per generation. No hidden fees, ever.

**The router is the cost story:**
Each step runs on the cheapest model that will succeed. You pay frontier-model prices only on the ~10–20% of steps that truly need them. That's why HireBuddha undercuts a flat "always-premium-LLM" bill while keeping premium quality exactly where it counts.
