## SECTION 2 — The 5-Tier Hierarchy

**Principle:** *Mental-model transfer.* Mapping AI tiers onto a human org chart (worker → manager → C-suite) lets a buyer understand a novel architecture instantly using knowledge they already trust.

## Built like a company. Because it runs like one.

A single giant prompt trying to do everything is how AI projects fail. HireBuddha separates strategy from execution across five tiers. Strategy stays at the top. Tool calls stay at the bottom. Each layer does exactly what it's built for – and nothing it isn't.

**The composition rule, bottom-up:** an Action wraps a Tool. Actions combine into a Skill. Skills into an Agent. Agents into a Process. Processes into a Loop.

| Tier | Think of it as | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Loop** | The whole department / company | A continuously running business function that never ends. Owns goals, KPIs, and schedules; orchestrates a portfolio of Processes. *Your whole company runs as one Loop: Sheel.* |
| **Process** | The C-suite orchestrator | Coordinates multi-department workflows on a DAG with parallel branches, conditional routing, and child-agent dispatch. |
| **Agent** | A manager / department head | Owns a communication channel, holds conversational state, applies your guidelines, routes hard cases to specialists. |
| **Skill** | A specialist | Executes one focused technical task – document generation, data analysis, template processing. |
| **Action** | An individual worker | One atomic, tool-assisted step. The thin interface between AI and your external systems. |

**Key message:**
This is why HireBuddha doesn't collapse under complexity the way single-prompt "agents" do. Strategy can't leak into a tool call. A tool call can't rewrite the strategy. The structure *is* the reliability.

## SECTION 4 — Execution Reasoning Modes

**Principle:** *Demonstrated competence.* Showing that the system chooses *how* to think (not just what to say) proves cognition. Engineers especially convert here.

## It doesn't just respond. It reasons – the right way for the task.

- **ReAct (Reason-then-Act).** The employee picks a tool, runs it, observes the result, and loops until done. Best for database operations and linear tasks.
- **Chain-of-Thought.** It writes out its step-by-step logic before producing the output. Best for drafting, analysis, and legal review.
- **Debate / Multi-Path.** Several candidate reasoning paths are generated and a critic selects or synthesizes the best. Reserved for high-stakes Process- and Loop-tier decisions.

**And every step is model-routed.** Whatever the mode, the Intelligent Router binds each step to the right brain at runtime – BabyBuddha for most work, a frontier model like Claude Opus when the task earns it. *(See Intelligence & Memory.)*
