## SECTION 4 — How We Build

**Principle:** *Credibility through values-in-action.* Showing the principles we engineer by (transparency, control, reversibility) reassures the buyer that the philosophy isn't just marketing.

- **Transparency.** Every cost, every model choice, every decision is logged and shown to you.
