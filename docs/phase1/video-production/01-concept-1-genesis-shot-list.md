## The Story in One Line

A point of golden light is born, grows a mind, hands, and a voice; becomes an employee of light; multiplies into seven loops orbiting a company; turns to face you as Pragya; scales into a city of autonomous businesses — and the whole universe turns out to be the reflection in the calm eye of a founder drinking chai.

## Clip-by-Clip

### CLIP 1 — "Birth" (8s, frames 1–192)
**Beat:** Hero. A single point of light is born in absolute darkness.
**Camera:** Static, dead-center composition, then an almost imperceptible slow push-in (≤5%).
**Conditioning:** Text-to-video (the only clip with no first-frame chain).

**Prompt:**
> A vast empty void of deep warm charcoal, almost black, with the faintest atmospheric haze. At the exact center, a single point of warm golden-amber light is born — first a spark, then a slow silent bloom, like the first star igniting. Delicate particles of light drift outward from it and are drawn back in, breathing. The light pulses gently, organically, like a heartbeat at rest. Extremely minimal, meditative, vast negative space. In the final two seconds, the light settles into a perfect steady glow at center frame and holds completely still.

**Hold frame HF-1:** Single steady golden point of light, dead center, faint halo, vast dark negative space — maximum room for the H1 headline above/around it.
**DOM sync:** "Stop Hiring. Start Deploying." is visible from frame 1 (the page must say its line in the first 5 seconds regardless of scroll).

### CLIP 2 — "The Dissolution" (8s, frames 193–384)
**Beat:** Old Way / New Way. The old world's structure drifts in grey and dies into the light.
**Camera:** Slow lateral drift right (the grey enters from the left, the light holds right-of-center).
**Conditioning:** First frame = HF-1. Reference: none.

**Prompt:**
> Beginning from a single steady golden point of light in a dark charcoal void: from the left side of frame, a tangled three-dimensional wireframe slowly drifts in — a chaotic corporate org chart made of dull desaturated grey lines and boxes, knotted, heavy, slightly rusted, sagging under its own weight. As it drifts toward the golden light, the wireframe begins to unravel strand by strand; each grey line touched by the light's glow dissolves into fine golden particles that spiral gracefully into the light, feeding it. The light grows brighter and slightly larger as it consumes the structure. Melancholy turning into warmth. In the final two seconds, the last grey fragment dissolves, leaving the golden light — now twice as bright — alone right-of-center, holding still.

**Hold frame HF-2:** Brighter golden orb right-of-center; faint dissolving particle trails; left half of frame clear and dark — the DOM's "Old Way" column sits over the dark left, "New Way" beside the light.
**DOM sync:** Old-way stats (90 days / ₹35 lakh / 2.3 years) stamp in over the grey tangle while it's still visible (12%); new-way stats (10 minutes / $5/day / 24·7·365) appear beside the brightened light (15%).

### CLIP 3 — "The Mind" (8s, frames 385–576)
**Beat:** Pillar 1 — AI Employees ("Not chatbots. Cognitive workers.")
**Camera:** Slow push-in toward the orb, ending in a medium shot of the new figure.
**Conditioning:** First frame = HF-2. Last frame = generated target matching REF-A (figure, three-quarter view). Reference images: REF-A.

**Prompt:**
> Beginning from a bright golden orb of light in a dark charcoal void: the orb stretches and unfurls vertically, particles cascading downward like silk, assembling into an elegant abstract humanoid figure of golden light particles — translucent, graceful, no face, no defined gender, standing calmly. As the body completes, a luminous lattice ignites inside its head and chest: an intricate branching neural structure of brighter gold filaments, pulsing with slow waves of thought, like synapses firing in slow motion. Each pulse travels from the head down through the body. The figure breathes. Awe, intelligence, calm. In the final two seconds the figure stands perfectly poised in three-quarter view, neural lattice glowing steadily, and holds.

**Hold frame HF-3:** The figure at right-third, neural lattice visible, left two-thirds dark — Pillar Card 1 renders left.
**DOM sync:** Card 1 (🧠 AI Employees) enters at 23% as the lattice ignites.

### CLIP 4 — "Hands and a Voice" (8s, frames 577–768)
**Beat:** Pillars 2–3 — Loops + Pay-per-outcome.
**Camera:** Slow orbital arc around the figure, ~45°, ending front-on.
**Conditioning:** First frame = HF-3. Reference: REF-A.

**Prompt:**
> A graceful abstract humanoid figure made of golden light particles stands in a dark charcoal void, a neural lattice glowing in its head and chest. Around the figure, small luminous geometric emblems materialize one by one and orbit it like tiny satellites — a glowing envelope, a telephone handset, a document, a calculator, a calendar, a speech bubble — each rendered as minimal indigo-and-gold light glyphs, NOT realistic objects and NOT text. One by one, each emblem glides inward and docks into the figure's forearms and hands, absorbed in a soft ripple of light. As the last one docks, the figure inclines its head and emits a single silent pulse from its chest: expanding concentric rings of golden sound-wave light that ripple outward through the void like rings on a pond. The camera arcs slowly around to face the figure head-on. In the final two seconds the rings fade, the figure stands front-on, palms slightly open, fully equipped, and holds.

**Hold frame HF-4:** Figure front-on, center-right, faint residual rings; tools now visible as subtle glowing seams on its forearms.
**DOM sync:** Card 2 (🔄 7 Loops) at 31% while emblems orbit; Card 3 (💰 Pay-per-outcome) at 34% on the voice pulse. Dead zone 37–41% holds all three cards.

### CLIP 5 — "Design → Deploy → Employ" (8s, frames 769–960)
**Beat:** The lifecycle. Three distinct activation pulses, one per step — the DOM steps sync to them.
**Camera:** Slow pull-back from medium to wide.
**Conditioning:** First frame = HF-4. Reference: REF-A.
**Internal timing (critical):** pulse 1 at ~1.5s, pulse 2 at ~4s, pulse 3 at ~6.5s. These map to Steps 1/2/3 in the DOM.

**Prompt:**
> A fully formed humanoid figure of golden light particles stands front-on in a dark charcoal void. THREE distinct events happen in sequence, evenly spaced. FIRST: a fine vertical scan-line of white-gold light sweeps down the figure from head to toe, and as it passes, the figure's particle body sharpens into crisp definition — it is being finalized. SECOND: from the darkness on both sides, thin elegant lines of indigo light extend horizontally toward the figure like circuits reaching out, connecting to its shoulders, hands and chest; where they connect, small nodes of light flare — channels plugging in. THIRD: the figure's entire form flares brighter for one beat and the indigo lines begin to flow with traveling pulses of light, streaming outward from the figure into the darkness in both directions — it is now working. The camera pulls back slowly throughout, revealing more of the void and the network lines extending beyond frame. In the final two seconds: wide shot, figure at center, six indigo channel-lines flowing steadily outward, holding.

**Hold frame HF-5:** Wide symmetric shot — figure center, flowing channel lines left and right; the DOM's horizontal 3-step flow sits in the lower third.
**DOM sync:** Step 1 "Design" appears on the scan-line; Step 2 "Deploy" on the channel connection; Step 3 "Employ" on the flow ignition. CTA "See it in action →" in the dead zone.

### CLIP 6 — "One Becomes Seven" (8s, frames 961–1152)
**Beat:** The 7 Loops. The signature shot of the whole film.
**Camera:** Pull-back + slow crane up, ending in an elevated three-quarter view of the orbital system.
**Conditioning:** First frame = HF-5. Last frame = generated target matching REF-C exactly (this composition must match the site's interactive orbital diagram — design them together). Reference: REF-A, REF-C.

**Prompt:**
> A humanoid figure of golden light stands at the center of a dark void, connected to flowing indigo channel-lines. The figure raises its arms slightly, and divides — gracefully, like a cell, like a dancer stepping out of themselves — into seven identical smaller figures of golden light. Each of the seven glides outward along a curved path, and as each one travels, it leaves behind a continuous elliptical trail of light, forming seven elliptical orbits at different inclinations around the center point — like an atom, like a solar system. Each orbit glows a slightly different warmth of gold. Where the original figure stood, a luminous faceted crystalline structure rises and assembles — abstract, architectural, suggesting a company headquarters built of light. The seven figures keep moving along their orbits, each a small bright comet. Camera cranes up and back to an elevated three-quarter view. In the final two seconds the system settles into steady, clockwork rotation — seven glowing orbits around one radiant center — and holds.

**Hold frame HF-6:** The complete orbital system, elevated three-quarter view, all 7 orbits readable as distinct paths. **This frame must be pixel-matched to the interactive diagram's resting state** — at 63% scroll the video crossfades into the real clickable SVG/Canvas diagram. The film becomes the product here.
**DOM sync:** Loop labels (Growth, Customer Success, Fulfillment, Finance, Compliance, Talent, Intelligence) fade in one-by-one pinned to their orbits as each forms; ICP self-select line appears in the dead zone.

### CLIP 7 — "She Sees You" (8s, frames 1153–1344)
**Beat:** Meet Pragya. The fourth wall bends.
**Camera:** Slow push-in from the wide orbital view, descending to eye level with one figure.
**Conditioning:** First frame = HF-6. Reference: REF-B (the violet Pragya variant), REF-C.

**Prompt:**
> An elevated view of seven golden orbits of light circling a radiant crystalline center in a dark void, seven small figures of light traveling the orbits. The camera begins a slow descent and push-in toward one of the orbiting figures. As the camera approaches, this one figure gently slows, steps off its orbit, and its golden particles shift hue to a soft luminous violet with a warm gold core. The violet figure turns to face the camera directly — calm, attentive, present — and inclines its head in a small, unmistakable gesture of greeting. Behind it, softly out of focus, the other six golden orbits continue their steady rotation. The violet figure raises one hand, palm up, and a small constellation of light blooms above its palm — an offering. In the final two seconds: medium shot, the violet figure centered facing the viewer, palm raised with the glowing constellation, the orbital system bokeh behind, holding.

**Hold frame HF-7:** Pragya front-on at center-left, palm offering light; right half reserved for the embedded chat widget.
**DOM sync:** Widget embeds at 70% into the space beside her raised palm — composition makes her appear to be *presenting* the widget. Subtext line at 73%. This is also the natural trigger for the ambient Pragya prompt if not yet shown.

### CLIP 8 — "A City of Light" (8s, frames 1345–1536)
**Beat:** Trust bar + customer stories. Scale-out: this is already happening everywhere.
**Camera:** Continuous pull-back and crane-up, the boldest camera move in the film.
**Conditioning:** First frame = HF-7. Reference: REF-C.

**Prompt:**
> A violet figure of light stands facing the camera with golden orbits rotating behind it in a dark void. The camera begins a long, smooth, accelerating pull-back and rise. As it pulls away, the orbital system shrinks — and other identical systems of golden orbits fade into view around it, first three, then dozens, then hundreds, each one a glowing seven-ringed mandala of light around its own crystalline center, scattered across a vast dark plane like a city of luminous atoms seen from a night flight. They vary subtly in size and warmth. Thin filaments of indigo light connect some of them, a quiet web of commerce. The vast charcoal darkness above holds faint warm haze, like dawn approaching. In the final two seconds the camera settles into a high, wide, breathtaking aerial view of the city of orbital lights, gently twinkling, and holds.

**Hold frame HF-8:** Aerial city-of-orbits, slightly darker upper third — logos, metric counters, and the three story cards sit in DOM panels over this twinkling field.
**DOM sync:** Metric counters (7+ industries, 20+ tools, millions of voice minutes) animate at 80%; customer story cards (Durwankur, Saraswat Bank, GoChillao) rise at 83% — each card visually anchored to one glowing system in the field (a subtle line connector if feasible).

### CLIP 9 — "The Founder's Eye" (6s, frames 1537–1632)
**Beat:** Final CTA. The reveal: all of this was a reflection.
**Camera:** Match-dissolve trick (see below), then slow pull-back from extreme close-up.
**Conditioning:** First frame = HF-8. Last frame = REF-D. Reference: REF-D. **Face allowed in this clip only.**

**Prompt:**
> A vast aerial field of glowing golden orbital systems twinkling in darkness. The camera pushes slowly forward and the field of lights softly defocuses into a wash of warm golden bokeh — and the bokeh resolves, seamlessly, into the reflection on the surface of a human eye in extreme close-up: a calm, warm brown iris, the golden lights mirrored in its curve. The camera pulls back slowly. A person in their thirties, relaxed, sits by a window at dusk holding a steaming cup of chai with both hands, face lit by the warm glow of an unseen screen, an expression of complete calm and quiet confidence — not working, just watching. Steam rises from the cup. Outside the window, a soft dusk skyline. In the final two seconds, they take an unhurried breath, almost a smile, and the shot holds in stillness.

**Hold frame HF-9:** The calm founder at dusk with chai, warm and human after 60 seconds of light and geometry — the emotional landing pad under "Your AI workforce is ready. Are you?"
**DOM sync:** Final headline at 93%, three CTAs at 95%, footer reachable in the last dead zone.
**Production note:** If the bokeh→eye match-dissolve fights you in one clip, split it: end Clip 8 on a defocused bokeh frame (alternate HF-8b) and start Clip 9 from a matching bokeh still using first+last frame interpolation toward REF-D. Casting note for REF-D: Indian founder, ambiguous age 28–40, no brand-identifiable clothing.
